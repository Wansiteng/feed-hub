彭博情报：DeepSeek 取得进展后，美国对中国的 AI 领先优势收窄

Bloomberg Technology · 评分 7/10：中美 AI 竞争格局的实质分析，值得读
原标题：US Lead in AI Over China Narrows After DeepSeek Gains, BI Says

要点

  • 中国顶尖模型在 benchmark 得分上仅落后美国对手 3%
  • 差距从 5 月的约 9% 和年初的 15% 大幅收窄
  • DeepSeek 发布 V4.1 Flash 后中国模型表现提升

American AI companies’ performance lead over China narrowed sharply in past months to a record low after labs such as DeepSeek gained ground, threatening US tech supremacy, according to Bloomberg Intelligence.

据Bloomberg Intelligence称，随着DeepSeek等实验室取得进展，美国AI公司对中国的性能领先优势在过去几个月中大幅收窄至历史最低水平，对美国的技术霸主地位构成威胁。

Top Chinese models lag their US rivals by just 3% on benchmark scores after the September release of DeepSeek’s V4.1 Flash, BI senior analyst Robert Lea wrote in a report Monday. That’s down from about 9% in May and 15% earlier in the year. That improving performance spells further market share gains for Chinese contenders, he said.

BI高级分析师Robert Lea在周一的一份报告中写道，在DeepSeek于9月发布V4.1 Flash之后，中国顶尖模型在benchmark得分上仅落后美国竞争对手3%。这一差距较5月份约9%和今年早些时候的15%有所缩小。他表示，性能的不断提升意味着中国竞争者将进一步抢占市场份额。
