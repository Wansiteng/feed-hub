iPad mini 8 将带来这 10 项新功能

MacRumors · 评分 7/10：A20 Pro 与 2nm 芯片细节，读者关注 Apple Silicon 新品
原标题：iPad Mini 8 to Offer These 10 New Features

要点

  • 下一代 iPad mini 将搭载 A20 Pro 芯片，采用台积电 2nm 工艺
  • A20 Pro 的 CPU 性能约为 A17 Pro 的 1.5 倍，GPU 约 2.5 倍
  • 新机内存升至 12GB，并首次采用 OLED 屏幕和横向前置摄像头

Last week, our exclusive leak revealed that the next-generation iPad mini will feature Apple's latest A20 Pro chip, a landscape front camera, and a redesigned speaker system, but that is not all, as plenty of other upgrades have been rumored.

上周，我们的独家爆料显示，下一代 iPad mini 将搭载 Apple 最新的 A20 Pro 芯片、横向前置摄像头以及重新设计的扬声器系统，但这还不是全部，据传还有大量其他升级。




Here are 10 new features and changes expected for the iPad mini 8:

以下是 iPad mini 8 预计将带来的 10 项新功能和变化：




  • Apple's latest A20 Pro chip, manufactured with TSMC's advanced 2nm process. Expect up to around 1.5× faster CPU performance and up to around 2.5× faster GPU performance compared to the A17 Pro chip in the current iPad mini. A20 Pro also introduces a "dual 16-core Neural Engine" for a total of 32 cores.
    Apple 最新的 A20 Pro 芯片，采用台积电先进的 2nm 工艺制造。与当前 iPad mini 中的 A17 Pro 芯片相比，CPU 性能预计提升约 1.5 倍，GPU 性能预计提升约 2.5 倍。A20 Pro 还引入了“双 16 核 Neural Engine”，总计 32 核。

  • With the A20 Pro chip, the next iPad mini will have 12GB of RAM, up from 8GB of RAM in the current model. This means the device will support the ability to customize the pace and expressiveness of Siri AI's voice.
    得益于 A20 Pro 芯片，下一代 iPad mini 将配备 12GB 内存，高于当前机型的 8GB 内存。这意味着该设备将支持自定义 Siri AI 语音的语速和表现力。

  • OLED display with better overall image quality compared to the current LCD model, thanks to richer colors and higher contrast ratio with true blacks.
    OLED 显示屏，相比当前的 LCD 机型，整体画质更佳，色彩更丰富，对比度更高，黑色更纯正。

  • Slimmer bezels around the display compared to the current model.
    相比当前机型，显示屏周围的边框更窄。

  • Landscape front camera, meaning the camera will be in the right bezel instead of the top bezel when holding the device in an upright position.
    横向前置摄像头，这意味着竖持设备时，摄像头将位于右侧边框，而非顶部边框。

  • Apple's custom-designed N1 chip with Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 support.
    Apple 自研的 N1 芯片，支持 Wi-Fi 7 和 Bluetooth 6。

  • Apple's custom-designed C1X or C2 modem for cellular configurations.
    Apple 自研的 C1X 或 C2 调制解调器，用于蜂窝网络配置。

  • A redesigned speaker system with no speaker holes and potentially an iPhone earpiece-like speaker in the top bezel.
    重新设计的扬声器系统，没有扬声器开孔，顶部边框中可能配备类似 iPhone 听筒的扬声器。

  • IP-rated water resistance for the first time.
    首次具备 IP 等级的防水性能。



Apple is expected to announce the iPad mini 8 this year, with an October launch most likely. In the U.S., the current iPad mini starts at $599 after receiving a $100 price increase in June, which Apple attributed to skyrocketing RAM and storage chip prices. With a costlier OLED display, the next model might be even more expensive.

Apple 预计将在今年发布 iPad mini 8，最有可能在 10 月推出。在美国，当前 iPad mini 的起售价为 599 美元，此前在 6 月涨价了 100 美元，Apple 将涨价归因于内存和存储芯片价格飙升。由于 OLED 显示屏成本更高，下一代机型可能还会更贵。
