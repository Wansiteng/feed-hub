Mac mini M6 评测：确实强悍，但如今更贵了

The Guardian · Technology · 评分 7/10：Apple Silicon 新品实测，读者关注 Mac 硬件
原标题：Mac mini M6 review: truly mighty but now more pricey

要点

  • Mac mini M6 起售价涨至 899 英镑，比 M4 版贵 300 英镑
  • M6 单核性能比 M4 快约 25%，多核快 50%，图形性能快约 75%
  • 机身仅 12.7 厘米见方、5 厘米厚，重 670 克，配备三个 Thunderbolt 4 接口

Apple’s latest Mac mini brings real speed and power for far more than everyday tasks in a practically pocketable form you can fit almost anywhere.

Apple 最新的 Mac mini 带来了真正的速度与性能，远不止应付日常任务，而且体积小巧到几乎可以放进口袋，随处安放。

The impressive new desktop machine is the first to receive an update to Apple’s latest M6 chip while remaining smaller than a headphones box. It is the cheapest computer Apple sells with an M-series chip, but like every other memory-containing piece of electronics, it has been hit by RAMageddon, driving up the starting price to £899 (€1,049/$899/A$1,449) from the £599 or equivalent of its M4 predecessor.

这款令人印象深刻的新台式机是首款搭载 Apple 最新 M6 芯片的产品，同时体积仍比一个耳机盒还小。它是 Apple 在售的最便宜的 M 系列芯片电脑，但和所有含内存的电子产品一样，它也遭遇了“内存末日”，起售价从 M4 前代的 599 英镑或等值价格，涨到了 899 英镑（1,049 欧元/899 美元/1,449 澳元）。

Apple introduced the Mac mini’s dainty form with 2024’s M4 model, which remains the same for the new M6 version. A lot has changed since the M2 Mac mini, which was pretty compact. The current mini measures just 12.7cm square and 5cm thick, with rounded corners and a solid aluminium body. It’s happy to sit on a desk, but is small and light enough to mount behind a monitor to turn any screen into a DIY iMac.

Apple 在 2024 年的 M4 机型上首次推出了 Mac mini 的小巧外形，新的 M6 版本保持不变。相比已经相当紧凑的 M2 Mac mini，变化很大。现款 mini 仅 12.7 厘米见方、5 厘米厚，圆角设计，一体成型铝金属机身。它放在桌面上毫无压力，但体积和重量都足够小，可以挂在显示器背后，把任何屏幕变成 DIY iMac。

The machine is fairly minimalist. A small LED on the front shows you when it’s on. The back has three Thunderbolt 4 (USB4) ports, a 2.5Gb ethernet socket, an HDMI 2.1 port, and a figure-eight power cable. It lacks any USB-A ports, but adapters are readily available for legacy devices not yet converted to USB-C, such as flash drives or printers.

这台机器相当极简。正面一个小 LED 灯显示开机状态。背面有三个 Thunderbolt 4（USB4）接口、一个 2.5Gb 以太网口、一个 HDMI 2.1 接口和一条八字形电源线。它没有任何 USB-A 接口，但对于尚未换成 USB-C 的旧设备，如闪存盘或打印机，转接头随处可买。

It has basic speakers built in, which are good enough for macOS’s notification sounds.

它内置了基础扬声器，用来播放 macOS 的通知音效足够了。

The mini runs the latest macOS 27 Golden Gate, which brings welcome refinements to Apple’s Liquid Glass design that was introduced last year. But the big update is a relaunch of Apple’s Siri assistant and AI tools. Siri AI, as it is now known, is vastly superior at understanding and performing tasks, such as finding information on your Mac or the web, and has a dedicated app to see previous interactions. It can read what’s on your screen, so you can ask it to track parcels from just a code in your email or find an object from an image with the CMD+Shift+Space keyboard shortcut.

mini 运行最新的 macOS 27 Golden Gate，对 Apple 去年推出的 Liquid Glass 设计做了令人欣喜的改进。但最大的更新是 Apple 的 Siri 助手和 AI 工具重新推出。如今名为 Siri AI 的它，在理解和执行任务方面强得多，比如在你的 Mac 或网络上查找信息，并有一个专门的应用来查看过往交互。它能读取你屏幕上的内容，所以你可以让它仅凭邮件里的一个单号追踪包裹，或用 CMD+Shift+Space 快捷键从图片中查找物品。

Siri is integrated into Spotlight search, which is handy but sometimes results in it trying to search the web instead of launching an app or a file on your Mac if you mistype, which can be frustrating.

Siri 已整合进 Spotlight 搜索，这很方便，但有时如果你打错字，它会试图搜索网络而不是启动 Mac 上的应用或文件，这可能会让人恼火。

Apple Intelligence is smarter too. Writing, proofing and summarising tools are built in and accessible from most apps, along with a collection of other features. The most surprisingly useful tool I found was file and folder naming suggestions, which made matching long file-naming schemes a doddle. Boring but very useful.

Apple Intelligence 也更聪明了。写作、校对和摘要工具已内置，在大多数应用中都能调用，还有一系列其他功能。我发现最出奇有用的是文件和文件夹命名建议，让匹配冗长的文件命名规则变得轻而易举。无聊但非常实用。

Serious M6 speed

强悍的 M6 速度

The Mac mini is offered with the newest Apple Silicon chip, the M6 (as tested) or with an M5 Pro, which has greater performance in multicore or graphics applications.

Mac mini 提供最新的 Apple Silicon 芯片，即 M6（本次测试机型）或 M5 Pro，后者在多核或图形应用中性能更强。

For most people the M6 is more than enough, with the fastest single core performance of any of Apple’s chips by about 8.5% in testing. That means it absolutely flies along with apps launching near instantly and tasks just flying by. Compared with the previous generation M4 Mac mini, the new machine is about 25% faster in single core tasks, 50% faster in multicore tasks with graphics performance about 75% faster. That makes it a giant leap over older machines coming to the end of their useful lives.

对大多数人来说，M6 已经绰绰有余，在测试中其单核性能是 Apple 所有芯片中最快的，领先约 8.5%。这意味着它运行起来绝对飞快，应用几乎瞬间启动，任务一闪而过。与上一代 M4 Mac mini 相比，新机器在单核任务中快约 25%，多核任务快 50%，图形性能快约 75%。这让它相比那些即将寿终正寝的老机器实现了巨大飞跃。

The machine is also power efficient and runs silently unless pushed to the limit, and even then is very quiet.

这台机器还很省电，除非被逼到极限，否则运行起来悄无声息，即便满载也非常安静。

Sustainability

可持续性

The Mac mini is made with 50% recycled materials. Apple breaks down the computer’s environmental impact in its report. Apple offers trade-in and free recycling schemes, including for non-Apple products.

Mac mini 采用 50% 再生材料制造。Apple 在其报告中详细说明了这台电脑的环境影响。Apple 提供以旧换新和免费回收计划，包括非 Apple 产品。

Price

价格

The Mac mini M6 costs from £899 (€1,049/$899/A$1,449) with 16GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.

Mac mini M6 起售价为 £899（€1,049/$899/A$1,449），配备 16GB 内存和 256GB 存储。

For comparison, the A18 Pro MacBook Neo costs from £699, the M5 MacBook Air starts at £1,299, the M4 iMac starts at £1,499 and the Mac Studio costs from £2,499.

作为对比，A18 Pro MacBook Neo 起售价为 699 英镑，M5 MacBook Air 起售价为 1,299 英镑，M4 iMac 起售价为 1,499 英镑，Mac Studio 起售价为 2,499 英镑。

Verdict

评测结论

The Mac mini is a fantastic general computer squeezed into a tiny box that fits just about anywhere. The new M6 chip has buckets of power and performance for practically anything. If you know you need more graphics performance the M5 Pro chip is also available, but at a significant cost.

Mac mini 是一台出色的通用电脑，被塞进了一个几乎可以放在任何地方的小巧机身里。全新的 M6 芯片拥有充沛的性能，几乎能应对任何任务。如果你明确需要更强的图形性能，也可以选择 M5 Pro 芯片，但价格要高出不少。

The port selection is very good for a machine of this size, only lacking traditional USB-A ports of those commonly needed. With three Thunderbolt 4 (USB4) ports it’s easy to add a dock or hub if you really need more connectivity.

对于这样一台小机器来说，接口配置非常出色，只缺少了常见的传统 USB-A 接口。它配备三个 Thunderbolt 4（USB4）接口，如果你确实需要更多连接，接上扩展坞或集线器也很方便。

The only downside is the RAMageddon-linked price hike that makes the Mac mini M6 slightly less of an instant recommendation than its predecessor. At £899 (€1,049/$899/A$1,449), it is still a lot of Mac for the money, just not the total bargain the M4 version used to be.

唯一的缺点是受“内存末日”影响的涨价，这让 Mac mini M6 不像前代那样值得毫不犹豫地推荐。售价 899 英镑（1,049 欧元/899 美元/1,449 澳元），它依然是物有所值的 Mac，只是不再像 M4 版本那样超值了。

Pros: very fast M6 chip, quiet and cool running, plenty of ports, headphones socket, 2.5GbE, wifi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread support.

Cons: price hike, no expandable/replaceable storage or memory, no USB-A ports, no SD card slot, only 256GB of storage in base model, power button on base is difficult to reach.

优点：M6 芯片速度极快，运行安静凉爽，接口丰富，有耳机插孔，2.5GbE，wifi 7，蓝牙 6 和 Thread 支持。

缺点：涨价，存储和内存不可扩展/更换，没有 USB-A 接口，没有 SD 卡槽，基础型号仅有 256GB 存储，底部电源键难以够到。
