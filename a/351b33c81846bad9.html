Meta 开源代码，让你打造 Muse AI 小设备

The Verge · AI · 评分 6/10：Meta 开源 AI agent 硬件代码，与 agent 生态相关，有实质信息
原标题：Meta open sources code to let you make Muse AI gadgets

要点

  • Meta 开源代码，允许用户自制搭载 Muse AI agent 的硬件设备
  • 支持 ESP32 开发板和树莓派，可连接屏幕、按钮、传感器等外设
  • Meta 免费发放 5000 个 Muse Home Link，本月开始发货，现可加入等待名单

Meta now lets you make your own Muse gadgets that feature the company’s new AI agent with code that the company open sourced. The company suggests projects like loading Muse on a color E Ink display to show reminders, adding it to an HDMI stick so you can display Muse on a big screen, or putting Muse on a small touchscreen device to make what looks kind of like a DIY Muse Charm.

Meta 现在允许你自制 Muse 小工具，这些设备搭载了该公司全新的 AI agent，所用代码已由公司开源。Meta 建议的项目包括：把 Muse 装到彩色电子墨水屏上显示提醒事项，将其接入 HDMI 电视棒以便在大屏幕上显示 Muse，或者把 Muse 装到小型触屏设备上，做出一个看起来有点像 DIY 版 Muse Charm 的东西。

“Muse gadgets are open source devices you build yourself,” Meta says. “Program an off-the-shelf ESP32 board or set up a Raspberry Pi with our SDKs, then connect Muse to your displays, buttons, sensors, actuators, and whatever else you’ve got lying on your workbench.” However, Meta cautions that you should “Proceed at your own risk!”

“Muse 小工具是你自己动手搭建的开源设备，”Meta 表示。“用我们的 SDK 给一块现成的 ESP32 开发板编程，或者配置一台 Raspberry Pi，然后把 Muse 连接到你的显示屏、按钮、传感器、执行器，以及工作台上其他任何你能找到的东西。”不过 Meta 也提醒说：“风险自负！”

The company is also giving away a Muse Home Link gadget that it created. With the Muse Home Link, you use community-built skills to have the AI agent “turn on a light, control your TV, send a document to a printer, and more, depending on your setup,” Meta says. It manufactured 5,000 of the gadgets, according to Meta Superintelligence Labs’ Nat Friedman, and you can claim a waitlist spot for the Muse Home Link now ahead of shipping sometime this month.

该公司还免费赠送一款它自己打造的 Muse Home Link 小工具。Meta 表示，借助 Muse Home Link，你可以使用社区构建的技能，让 AI agent“开灯、控制电视、把文档发送到打印机等等，具体取决于你的设备配置”。据 Meta Superintelligence Labs 的 Nat Friedman 称，这款小工具共生产了 5,000 台，你现在就可以申请加入 Muse Home Link 的等候名单，产品预计本月内发货。
