Taylor Swift《The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore》全16首曲目排名

Billboard · 评分 6/10：Taylor Swift新专辑曲目盘点，一线歌手大事但属榜单推荐
原标题：Taylor Swift’s Full ‘The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore’: All 16 Tracks Ranked

要点

  • Taylor Swift 宣布豪华版专辑 The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore，新增 4 首附赠曲目
  • 原版专辑 The Life of a Showgirl 于 2025 年 10 月 3 日发行，共 12 首歌曲
  • Billboard 将原版 12 首与豪华版 4 首合并，对全部 16 首曲目进行排名

Update (Sept. 29, 2026): Less than two weeks shy of the one-year anniversary of The Life of a Showgirl, Swift surprised fans by revealing that — despite her previous declaration that the album would be limited to exactly 12 songs — she would be releasing four bonus tracks as part of a deluxe edition titled The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore. Shortly after their respective releases, Billboard ranked the songs on both sets — and now, we’re combining this initial ranking (written by Jason Lipshutz) with the deluxe ranking (written by Hannah Dailey) to re-examine the Showgirl era in all its glittering glory. See our comprehensive ranking of all 16 Life of a Showgirl era tracks below.

更新（2026年9月29日）：距离《The Life of a Showgirl》一周年纪念日不到两周之际，Swift给粉丝们带来了惊喜——尽管她此前曾宣称这张专辑将严格限定为12首歌曲，但她将推出四首附加曲目，作为名为《The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore》的豪华版的一部分。在各自发行后不久，Billboard 对两张专辑中的歌曲进行了排名——现在，我们将这一初始排名（由Jason Lipshutz撰写）与豪华版排名（由Hannah Dailey撰写）相结合，重新审视Showgirl时代的璀璨辉煌。请见下方我们对全部16首Life of a Showgirl时代曲目的综合排名。

The wait is over. Back on Aug. 13, 2025, Taylor Swift went on her then-boyfriend Travis Kelce’s podcast New Heights to first reveal that her much-anticipated 12th album — to be titled The Life of a Showgirl, and to consist of 12 pop “bangers” co-written and co-produced with her old 1989 collaborators Max Martin and Shellback — would be arriving on Oct. 3. Now, roughly seven weeks and exactly one engagement announcement later, your English teacher is here to make her Showgirl debut.

等待结束了。早在2025年8月13日，Taylor Swift做客她当时的男友Travis Kelce的播客New Heights，首次透露她备受期待的第12张专辑——定名为The Life of a Showgirl，由12首流行“劲歌”组成，与她1989时期的老搭档Max Martin和Shellback共同创作和制作——将于10月3日发行。如今，大约七周之后，且恰好在一则订婚公告之后，你们的英语老师在此献上她的Showgirl首秀。

As the dozen-track set arrives this Friday — with just one featured guest, in fellow superstar and Eras Tour opener Sabrina Carpenter on the title track, though pop icon George Michael is also invoked via interpolation on “Father Figure” — Swift will also premiere her new film event, titled Taylor Swift: The Official Release Party of a Showgirl. The film reportedly features the music video for the album’s opener and lead single, “The Fate of Ophelia,” along with lyric videos for the other tracks and Swift’s own insight on the songs.

随着这张12首歌的专辑于本周五发行——仅有一位客串嘉宾，即同为超级巨星和Eras Tour开场嘉宾的Sabrina Carpenter，出现在同名曲中，不过流行偶像George Michael也通过《Father Figure》的插值被提及——Swift还将首映她的新电影活动，名为Taylor Swift: The Official Release Party of a Showgirl。据报道，该影片包含专辑开场曲兼首支单曲《The Fate of Ophelia》的音乐视频，以及其他曲目的歌词视频和Swift本人对这些歌曲的见解。

The twin releases should kick off a massive October for the megastar, with pop fans and chartwatchers around the world on high alert to see what kind of further Billboard history she can add to her already-staggering list of achievements. But before all of that, we present our rankings of Showgirl‘s 12 songs — which Swift said during her New Heights appearance that she would not be adding any additional bonus cuts to. (You can also read our full review of the new album here.)

这两项发行应该会为这位超级巨星开启一个盛大的十月，全球流行乐迷和榜单观察者都高度警惕，看她能在已经令人瞠目的成就清单上再添怎样的Billboard历史纪录。但在这一切之前，我们呈现对Showgirl 12首歌曲的排名——Swift在New Heights节目中曾表示她不会添加任何额外的附赠曲目。（你也可以在这里阅读我们对这张新专辑的完整评论。）
