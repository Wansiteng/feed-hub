苹果发布会定在 10 月 13 日？

MacRumors · 评分 6/10：苹果新品发布会日期与产品线爆料，有实质信息
原标题：Apple Event on October 13?

要点

  • 苹果计划10月13日发布全新智能家居中枢、第二代HomePod mini和新Apple TV 4K
  • Gruber认为全新家居中枢必须通过主题演讲视频和媒体活动亮相
  • 苹果可能在10月13日举办线上发布会并安排媒体上手体验

Earlier this week, Daring Fireball's John Gruber floated Tuesday, October 13 as a potential date for Apple to hand out iPhone Duo review units to selected reviewers in person, and it appears that he might have been on to something.

本周早些时候，Daring Fireball 的 John Gruber 提出 10 月 13 日星期二可能是 Apple 向选定评测者当面发放 iPhone Duo 评测机的日期，看来他的猜测或许有几分道理。




Bloomberg's Mark Gurman today reported that Apple plans to unveil its all-new smart home hub, a second-generation HomePod mini, and a new Apple TV 4K model on October 13, the same date that Gruber mentioned in his speculative piece a few days ago.

Bloomberg 的 Mark Gurman 今天报道称，Apple 计划于 10 月 13 日发布其全新的智能家居中枢、第二代 HomePod mini 以及新款 Apple TV 4K，这与 Gruber 几天前在推测文章中提到的日期相同。



In a separate post, Gruber opined that there is "no way" that Apple would unveil the home hub through an Apple Newsroom announcement only, given it is an all-new device with an all-new interface. "If this is coming, it has to be unveiled with a keynote video and some sort of media event with hands-on demos," he said.

在另一篇文章中，Gruber 认为 Apple“绝不可能”仅通过 Apple Newsroom 公告来发布这款家居中枢，因为它是一款拥有全新界面的全新设备。他说：“如果这款产品要推出，就必须通过主题演讲视频以及某种带有上手演示的媒体活动来发布。”



If Gruber is right that the home hub "has to be unveiled with a keynote video," that would set the stage for an online-only Apple Event that would be streamed on Apple's website and YouTube on October 13. Afterwards, Apple would likely hold a hands-on experience for the media, allowing them to demo the products that were announced.

如果 Gruber 所说的家居中枢“必须通过主题演讲视频来发布”属实，那就意味着 10 月 13 日将举办一场仅在线上举行的 Apple Event，在 Apple 官网和 YouTube 上直播。之后，Apple 可能会为媒体举办上手体验活动，让他们试用发布的产品。



The same thing happened in October 2023. On October 24 that year, Apple announced that it would be holding an online-only Apple Event on October 30, which was streamed on Apple's website and YouTube. Later that day, Apple allowed selected journalists and content creators to go hands-on with the Macs unveiled during the event in person.

2023 年 10 月也发生过同样的情况。当年 10 月 24 日，Apple 宣布将于 10 月 30 日举办一场仅在线上举行的 Apple Event，在 Apple 官网和 YouTube 上直播。当天晚些时候，Apple 允许选定的记者和内容创作者亲自上手体验活动中发布的 Mac。



During the hands-on experience, selected individuals could also receive an iPhone Duo review unit in hand, allowing Apple to avoid a first-of-its-kind iPhone getting lost in the mail before it launches on Friday, October 23. Apple typically gives out review units around a week before a device launches, so the timing would line up.

在上手体验期间，选定的人士还可以当场拿到 iPhone Duo 评测机，这样 Apple 就能避免这款史无前例的 iPhone 在 10 月 23 日星期五上市前寄丢。



In any case, mark your calendars, as it sounds like October 13 will be a big day for Apple.

无论如何，请在日历上做好标记，因为 10 月 13 日听起来会是 Apple 的大日子。



If there is an October 13 event, expect Apple to announce it by next week.

如果 10 月 13 日真有活动，预计 Apple 会在下周之前公布。
