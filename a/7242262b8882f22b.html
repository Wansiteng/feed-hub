中国发布五年电池规划，目标 2030 年实现全固态电池大规模应用

原标题：China unveils 5-year battery plan targeting large-scale all-solid-state use by 2030

要点

  • 中国发布2026-2030年电池产业五年规划，目标2030年实现全固态电池初步规模化应用
  • 规划由工信部等7部门联合发布，要求长寿命锂电池循环次数达15000次
  • 规划提出以锂电池为主、钠电池和液流电池为辅的供应体系，支持企业兼并重组
  • China has prioritized breakthroughs in all-solid-state technologies to accelerate the roll-out of next-generation batteries.
  • The plan also calls for improving sodium battery performance and supporting corporate mergers and restructuring.
  • 中国将全固态技术突破列为优先事项，以加速下一代电池的推出。
  • 该方案还要求提升钠电池性能，并支持企业兼并重组。

China aims to achieve initial large-scale use of all-solid-state batteries by 2030, accelerating the transition of next-generation battery technology from research to commercialization.

中国计划到2030年实现全固态电池的初步规模化应用，加速下一代电池技术从研发走向商业化。

The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) on Monday released its new battery industry development plan for the fifteenth five-year period, setting a roadmap for 2026 through 2030.

工信部周一发布了十五五时期新型电池产业发展规划，为2026年至2030年设定了路线图。

The document, dated September 14, was jointly issued by 7 government agencies, including MIIT, the National Development and Reform Commission and the Ministry of Transport.

该文件日期为9月14日，由工信部、国家发展改革委和交通运输部等7个政府部门联合印发。

The plan calls for steady growth in China's new battery industry through 2030, alongside breakthroughs in advanced electrode materials, new electrolytes and high-end auxiliary materials.

该方案提出，到2030年中国新型电池产业将稳步增长，同时在先进电极材料、新型电解质和高端辅助材料方面取得突破。

Beyond the all-solid-state battery deployment target, it sets goals for long-life lithium batteries to reach 15,000 charge-discharge cycles and for leading manufacturers to achieve product defect rates at the parts-per-billion (PPB) level.

除了全固态电池部署目标外，该方案还设定了长寿命锂电池达到15000次充放电循环，以及领先制造商实现产品缺陷率达到十亿分之一（PPB）级别的目标。

All-solid-state batteries are a key technological priority. The document calls for faster breakthroughs in commercialization technologies, focusing on improving ionic conductivity and cycling stability while optimizing costs.

全固态电池是关键技术优先方向。文件要求加快商业化技术突破，重点提升离子电导率和循环稳定性，同时优化成本。

Specific measures include improving interfacial contact, extending cycle life, advancing pressure-system engineering and scaling up production of high-performance solid electrolytes.

具体措施包括改善界面接触、延长循环寿命、推进压力系统工程，以及扩大高性能固体电解质的规模化生产。

The plan also calls for combining high-voltage, high-capacity cathode materials with new lithium-metal-based anodes and anode-free designs, while developing manufacturing equipment such as isostatic presses for solid-state batteries.

该方案还要求将高压、高容量正极材料与新型锂金属负极及无负极设计相结合，同时开发固态电池等静压机等制造设备。

China plans to build a supply system centered on lithium batteries, complemented by sodium and flow batteries, while strengthening its ability to commercialize all-solid-state and ultrafast-charging batteries.

中国计划构建以锂电池为中心、钠电池和液流电池为补充的供应体系，同时增强全固态电池和超快充电池的商业化能力。

For sodium batteries, the document emphasizes cost, safety and resource availability advantages, calling for the development of cold-resistant, high-energy-density power batteries and energy storage batteries with long cycle lives.

对于钠电池，文件强调其成本、安全和资源可得性优势，要求开发耐寒、高能量密度的动力电池和长循环寿命的储能电池。

Industry consolidation and supply chain security are another focus. The document supports corporate mergers and restructuring and calls for faster exploration and development of domestic lithium, cobalt and other mineral resources.

行业整合和供应链安全是另一个重点。文件支持企业兼并重组，并要求加快国内锂、钴等矿产资源的勘探开发。

On recycling, the plan requires companies to establish used-battery collection systems aligned with their sales volumes. It also encourages them to build recycling capacity in major export markets where conditions permit.

在回收方面，该方案要求企业建立与其销售量相匹配的废旧电池回收体系。还鼓励企业在条件允许的主要出口市场建设回收产能。

The document proposes a digital identity management system for products including new energy vehicle (NEV) power batteries, exploring internationally accepted battery passport arrangements and promoting mutual recognition of carbon footprint data.

该文件提出对新能源汽车（NEV）动力电池等产品实施数字身份管理系统，探索国际通行的电池护照安排，并推动碳足迹数据互认。

The battery plan follows China's September 11 release of its fifteenth five-year development plan for the intelligent connected NEV industry, which targets a 70% share for NEVs in domestic new passenger vehicle sales by 2030.

该电池方案出台之前，中国于9月11日发布了智能网联新能源汽车产业十五五发展规划，目标是到2030年新能源汽车在国内新乘用车销量中占比达到70%。

The earlier automotive plan also called for tighter management of power battery production capacity. The latest battery plan further defines priorities for technology development, manufacturing upgrades and broader applications.

较早的汽车规划还要求加强对动力电池产能的管理。最新的电池方案进一步明确了技术开发、制造升级和更广泛应用的重点。
