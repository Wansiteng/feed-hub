  • 尊界V800于8月5日上市，售价区间为76.6万元（113,710美元）至101.6万元。

Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp (JAC, SSE: 600418) shares fell by the daily limit on Thursday after reported brake pedal bracket failures during tests of its Huawei-backed Maextro V800 luxury MPV (multi-purpose vehicle) became a trending topic on Chinese social media.

安徽江淮汽车集团（江淮汽车，上交所：600418）股价周四跌停，此前有报道称，其与华为合作的尊界V800豪华MPV（多用途汽车）在测试中出现制动踏板支架断裂，相关话题在中国社交媒体上成为热点。

As of publication, JAC shares were at the daily lower limit, having ended the morning session at 24.72 yuan, down 10.01%. The decline reversed several consecutive days of gains.

截至发稿，江淮汽车股价处于跌停板，午间收盘报24.72元，下跌10.01%。此次下跌逆转了此前连续多日的涨势。

Dongchedi, an Chinese automotive information platform, released a test video earlier on Thursday saying that all 3 Maextro V800s suffered brake pedal bracket failures during emergency braking tests from 100 km/h to a standstill.

中国汽车信息平台懂车帝周四早些时候发布测试视频称，3辆尊界V800在100公里/小时至静止的紧急制动测试中均出现制动踏板支架断裂。

The first vehicle's bracket broke during its third braking attempt, and the second vehicle's during its fourth. A third new vehicle purchased subsequently suffered the same failure during its second attempt.

第一辆车在第三次制动尝试时支架断裂，第二辆车在第四次尝试时断裂。随后购买的第三辆新车在第二次尝试时也出现同样故障。

According to the video, the failures prevented normal braking using the pedal, forcing testers to stop the vehicles using the emergency braking function activated through the Park control.

据视频显示，故障导致无法通过踏板正常制动，测试人员被迫使用通过驻车控制激活的紧急制动功能将车辆停下。

The descriptions of the failures come from the testing team, and no official investigation findings have been released. JAC and Huawei's HIMA (Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance) have yet to comment on the matter.

上述故障描述来自测试团队，目前尚无官方调查结论发布。江淮汽车与华为的鸿蒙智行（Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance）均未就此置评。

The video sparked debate over vehicle safety and the testing methods. Some social media users called on JAC to investigate, while others questioned whether repeated high-intensity braking met applicable standards and whether they accurately reflected real-world driving conditions.

该视频引发了对车辆安全和测试方法的争论。一些社交媒体用户呼吁江淮汽车展开调查，另一些人则质疑反复高强度制动是否符合适用标准，以及是否准确反映真实驾驶条件。

Maextro, jointly developed by Huawei and JAC, sits at the top of HIMA's brand portfolio.

尊界由华为与江淮汽车联合开发，位居鸿蒙智行品牌矩阵的顶端。

The V800 was launched on August 5 as a flagship MPV, with announced launch prices ranging from 766,000 yuan ($113,710) to 1,016,000 yuan. It targets the high-end business mobility market that includes the Toyota Alphard and Lexus LM.

V800 于 8 月 5 日上市，定位旗舰 MPV，公布的上市价格区间为 76.6 万元（113,710 美元）至 101.6 万元。它瞄准的是包括丰田埃尔法和雷克萨斯LM在内的高端商务出行市场。

JAC has repeatedly said it will concentrate its resources on ensuring Maextro's commercial success. Chairman Xiang Xingchu has also said the company will continue investing in the ultra-luxury market and expand the brand's product lineup.

江淮汽车多次表示将集中资源确保尊界的商业成功。董事长项兴初也表示，公司将继续投资超豪华市场并扩展该品牌的产品阵容。

The limit-down move followed a string of gains in JAC shares. In an announcement on the evening of September 30, the company said the cumulative deviation of its daily closing-price gains had exceeded 20% over the 3 consecutive trading days of September 28, 29 and 30.

此次跌停之前，江淮汽车股价经历了一连串上涨。在9月30日晚间的公告中，公司称其9月28日、29日和30日连续3个交易日收盘价格涨幅累计偏离值已超过20%。

In the announcement, JAC confirmed that it had discussed potential cooperation with Huawei and Stellantis. However, as of the announcement's publication, neither the scope nor the form of cooperation had been determined, and no legally binding formal agreement had been signed.

公告中，江淮汽车确认已与华为和Stellantis讨论潜在合作。但截至公告发布，合作范围与形式均未确定，也未签署具有法律约束力的正式协议。

JAC said at the time that further progress on the potential cooperation remained subject to significant uncertainty.

江淮汽车当时表示，潜在合作的进一步进展仍存在重大不确定性。
