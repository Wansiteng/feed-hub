库珀·弗拉格有机会成为特别的存在

原标题：Cooper Flagg has the chance to be special

要点

  • 库珀·弗拉格上赛季后半段场均23分7篮板5助攻，多次砍下40+
  • 弗拉格上赛季三分命中率仅29%，场均出手3.5次，真实命中率排联盟第25百分位
  • 弗拉格新赛季目标应是入选全明星并竞争最佳阵容

The Dallas Mavericks enter the 2026-2027 season with numerous questions, but Cooper Flagg isn’t one them.

达拉斯独行侠带着诸多疑问进入2026-2027赛季，但库珀·弗拉格不在其中。

The 19-year old enters his second season with sky-high expectations, fueled by his incredible play in the back-half of last season.

这位19岁的球员带着极高的期望进入第二个赛季，这源于他在上赛季后半段的惊艳表现。

The jump from year one to year two is often massive, especially for generational talents such as Flagg. For example: LeBron James went from 20.9 points per game, to over 27 points per game, while also improving his efficiency. Obviously it is unfair to levy these expectations on Flagg, but most superstars show tremendous growth in their first real NBA offseason.

从第一年到第二年的飞跃往往是巨大的，尤其是对于弗拉格这样的世代级天才。例如：勒布朗·詹姆斯从场均20.9分提升到超过27分，同时效率也有所提高。显然，将这样的期望强加于弗拉格并不公平，但大多数超级巨星在第一个真正的NBA休赛期都会展现出巨大的进步。

Flagg averaged 23 points, 5 assists, and 7 rebounds in his final 51 games, including multiple 40+ point games. Flagg’s comfort level and confidence grew exponentially, quickly becoming one of the most dangerous downhill scorers in all of basketball.

弗拉格在最后51场比赛中场均得到23分、5次助攻和7个篮板，其中包括多场40+得分的比赛。弗拉格的舒适度和信心呈指数级增长，迅速成为全篮球界最危险的下坡得分手之一。

But at just 19-years of age, there is still ample room for growth. Flagg shot just 29% from three on the season, and often turned down open threes to settle for tougher shots. His defense was also somewhat inconsistent, as even though he averaged nearly 2 steals and blocks per game, the advanced metrics suggest he was only average on that end.

但年仅19岁，仍有充足的成长空间。弗拉格赛季三分命中率仅为29%，并且经常放弃空位三分，选择更难的投篮。他的防守也有些不稳定，尽管他场均接近2次抢断和盖帽，但高阶数据表明他在防守端仅处于平均水平。

Flagg’s potential is truly limitless, and he has already shown incredible development in his young career. All of this leads me to believe that a special season is ahead for the former number one overall pick.

弗拉格的潜力 truly 无限，他已经在年轻的职业生涯中展现了惊人的发展。所有这些让我相信，这位前状元秀将迎来一个特别的赛季。

This one is easy; Cooper Flagg has to shoot the ball better in 2026.

这一点很简单；库珀·弗拉格必须在2026年更好地投篮。

Flagg was an all-around bad shooter last season. He shot 29% from three with only attempting 3.5 threes per game. He also took far too many “long mid range” jumpers, which are defined as two point attempts that are attempted from farther than 10 feet.

弗拉格上赛季是一个全方位的糟糕射手。他三分命中率29%，场均仅出手3.5次三分。他还投了太多“长中距离”跳投，即距离超过10英尺的两分球尝试。

This combination of poor shot selection and poor shot-making cratered Flagg’s efficiency last season, as he was in the 25th percentile for league-wide true-shooting%.

糟糕的投篮选择和糟糕的投篮命中率相结合，导致弗拉格上赛季的效率暴跌，他的真实命中率仅排在联盟第25百分位。

This lack of shooting also effected his ability to get downhill, with many teams simply ignoring him on the perimeter to load up on his drives. His sheer unwillingness to attempt these shots was the biggest issue, as he would often settle for 18 footers rather than simply taking an open three.

投篮能力的缺乏也影响了他下坡攻击的能力，许多球队在外线直接放空他，以加强对他的突破防守。他完全不愿尝试这些投篮是最大的问题，因为他经常选择18英尺的跳投，而不是简单地投空位三分。

If Flagg can extend the defense to beyond the three-point line, he becomes truly un-guardable. We even saw an example of this last season against the Orlando Magic when Flagg scored 51 points. He made six threes in nine attempts, including a barrage or tough threes in the fourth quarter.

如果弗拉格能将防守扩展到三分线外，他将变得真正无法防守。我们甚至在上赛季对阵奥兰多魔术的比赛中看到了一个例子，当时弗拉格砍下51分。他三分球9投6中，包括第四节一连串的高难度三分。

Now I’m not going to expect a massive jump in efficiency, nor will I expect a massive jump in attempts. But if Flagg does not at least try and extend his range, his scoring potential will be capped underneath a hard-ceiling.

现在我不期望效率大幅提升，也不期望出手次数大幅增加。但如果弗拉格至少不尝试扩展射程，他的得分潜力将被限制在一个硬性上限之下。

Simply put, the best-case scenario for Cooper Flagg this season is joining the conversation for the best player in the world.

简而言之，库珀·弗拉格本赛季的最佳情况是加入世界最佳球员的讨论。

This may seem completely far-fetched, but Mavericks have seen this exact scenario play out before with Luka Doncic. Both players had record-breaking rookie seasons, and entered year two with sky-high expectations, with Doncic becoming a top-5 player in the world overnight.

这似乎完全难以置信，但独行侠之前已经见过这种情况在卢卡·东契奇身上发生。两位球员都拥有破纪录的新秀赛季，并带着极高的期望进入第二年，东契奇一夜之间成为世界前五的球员。

So how could Flagg reach that seemingly impossible level?

那么弗拉格如何才能达到那个看似不可能的水平呢？

Well it would take some significant development, but not it would not be impossible. First, Flagg would have to go from a bad shooter to a very good one. Outside of Giannis Antetokounmpo, all of the other players in the top 5 are elite shooters from either the three or mid-range. Flagg would have to double his three-point attempts while also increasing efficiency.

好吧，这需要一些重大的进步，但并非不可能。首先，弗拉格必须从一个糟糕的射手变成一个非常出色的射手。除了扬尼斯·阿德托昆博之外，前五名中的其他所有球员都是三分或中距离的顶级射手。弗拉格必须将自己的三分出手次数翻倍，同时还要提高效率。

A similar jump was seen with Jayson Tatum, as he went from attempting 3.9 3PA at 37%, to over 8 threes per game at 40% from his second to third season.

杰森·塔图姆也曾有过类似的飞跃，从第二个赛季到第三个赛季，他的三分出手从场均3.9次、命中率37%，提升到了场均超过8次、命中率40%。

Along with improvements in his shooting, Flagg would also have to improve his ball-handling. While Flagg did not produce many turnovers overall, he was top 10 in the league in lost balls that resulted in turnovers. These issues were exasperated when Flagg went up against the truly elite perimeter defenders in the league, as he often struggled to create space against these players.

除了投篮方面的进步，弗拉格还必须提升自己的控球能力。虽然弗拉格整体失误并不多，但他的丢球导致失误数排在联盟前十。当弗拉格面对联盟中真正顶级的外线防守者时，这些问题会被进一步放大，因为他常常难以在这些球员面前创造出空间。

Finally, Flagg would have to adjust to defenses having a year of film on his game, and deal with the amount of attention he will receive. I imagine defenses will try and pressure him as much as possible, throwing lots of bodies in his direction. If he has the composure to deal with this pressure consistently, defenses will be completely out-of-luck when playing the Mavericks.

最后，弗拉格还必须适应对手已经对他研究了一整年的比赛录像，并应对他将受到的关注度。我想防守方会尽可能对他施压，派大量人手朝他扑来。如果他能有足够的沉着来持续应对这种压力，那么防守方在对阵独行侠时就彻底没辙了。

The worse case scenario for Cooper Flagg is staying stagnant.

对库珀·弗拉格来说，最糟糕的情况就是停滞不前。

I think it’s very unlikely Flagg will regress in year 2, especially because he didn’t have unsustainable outside shooting.

我认为弗拉格在第二年退步的可能性非常小，尤其是因为他并没有不可持续的外线投篮表现。

If spent his entire summer at the beach this scenario could happen, otherwise, I don’t think its very likely.

如果他整个夏天都在海滩上度过，这种情况才可能发生，否则，我认为这不太可能。

The goal for Cooper Flagg in his sophomore season should be to make an all-star team, and compete for an All-NBA selection.

库珀·弗拉格在第二个赛季的目标应该是入选全明星阵容，并竞争最佳阵容的席位。

This may seem like a very high bar, but it really shouldn’t be that hard for Flagg to reach. If he’s able to slightly increase his volume and efficiency in both scoring and playmaking, being an all-star should be relatively easy.

这看起来可能是一个很高的标准，但对弗拉格来说，要达到这个目标其实并不应该那么难。如果他能在得分和组织两方面都略微提升产量和效率，入选全明星应该相对容易。

But past getting recognition from his peers and fans, another bar he needs to reach is in his leadership.

但除了获得同行和球迷的认可之外，他还需要达到另一个标准，那就是他的领导力。

Flagg is already a vocal player who plays with a tremendous amount of energy and hustle, so becoming the leader of this team shouldn’t be that hard. Obviously Kyrie Irving is the team’s leader, but he isn’t the future of the franchise.

弗拉格已经是一个敢于发声的球员，打球充满能量和拼劲，所以成为这支球队的领袖应该不会太难。显然，凯里·欧文是这支球队的领袖，但他并不是球队的未来。

Flagg doesn’t need to be a perfect leader as a 20-year-old, nor does it really matter for this season. But whether wants it or not, the team will be molded in his image, both in their playstyle, and the mentality of the team.

弗拉格不需要在20岁时就成为一个完美的领袖，这对本赛季来说也并不重要。但无论他愿不愿意，这支球队都会按照他的样子来塑造，无论是打法还是球队的心态。

It cannot be overstated how excited Mavericks fans should be for Cooper Flagg’s second season. He has every tool to be a superstar, and has shown the hunger to improve over his entire career.

独行侠球迷对库珀·弗拉格第二个赛季的期待再怎么强调都不为过。他具备成为超级巨星的一切条件，并且在整个职业生涯中都展现出了进步的渴望。

The roster construction could hold him back from truly reaching his potential this season, as he will more than likely be forced to play another season at point guard.

球队的阵容构建可能会在本赛季阻碍他真正发挥出自己的潜力，因为他很可能会被迫再打一个赛季的控球后卫。

But I’m confident Flagg will excel.

但我相信弗拉格会大放异彩。

Because it’s all he’s ever done.

因为这正是他一直都在做的事。
