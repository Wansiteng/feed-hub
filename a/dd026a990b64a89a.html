蔚来Q3交付量增长25%，但9月增速放缓至7.7%

Electrek · 评分 6/10：蔚来交付数据与品牌表现，电动车行业实质信息
原标题：NIO (NIO) Q3 deliveries rise 25%, but September growth slows to 7.7%

要点

  • 蔚来三季度交付 109,178 辆，同比增长 25.4%，落在指引区间内
  • 月交付仅增 7.7% 至 37,408 辆，为 2026 年最慢增速
  • ONVO 品牌三季度交付 27,728 辆，同比下滑 26.4%

NIO delivered 109,178 electric vehicles in the third quarter of 2026, a 25.4% increase over last year and right inside the 108,000 to 111,000 range it guided for in September.

蔚来在2026年第三季度交付了109,178辆电动汽车，同比增长25.4%，正好落在其9月给出的108,000至111,000辆指引区间内。

But the quarter ended on a weak note. September deliveries grew just 7.7% year-over-year to 37,408 units, the slowest pace of 2026, as the ONVO brand fell 43%.

但这一季度收尾疲软。9月交付量同比仅增长7.7%至37,408辆，为2026年最慢增速，其中ONVO品牌下滑43%。

A quarter that fades month after month

一个逐月走弱的季度

The Chinese EV maker released its September and Q3 numbers today. On the surface, it’s fine: NIO hit its own guidance, year-to-date deliveries are up 49.2% to 300,301 units, and the company has now delivered 1,297,893 vehicles since it started.

这家中国电动汽车制造商今天公布了9月和第三季度数据。表面上看还不错：蔚来达到了自己的指引，年初至今交付量增长49.2%至300,301辆，公司自成立以来累计交付量已达1,297,893辆。

The monthly trend is the problem. Growth was 71% in July, 14.5% in August, and 7.7% in September. Part of that is a tougher comparison, since NIO’s volume started ramping in the back half of 2025, but it’s also what’s happening on the ground with ONVO.

问题出在月度趋势上。7月增长71%，8月增长14.5%，9月增长7.7%。部分原因是基数更高，因为蔚来的销量从2025年下半年才开始放量，但这也与ONVO的实际表现有关。

We flagged the slowdown back in May, when NIO’s April deliveries came in up only 22.8% after a 98% first quarter. New flagships bailed out the second quarter. They didn’t do the same for the third.

我们在5月就曾指出这一放缓趋势，当时蔚来4月交付量仅增长22.8%，而第一季度增长了98%。新旗舰车型拯救了第二季度，但第三季度未能复制这一表现。

The NIO brand is carrying the whole company

蔚来品牌独撑大局

Break the quarter down by brand and you get three very different stories:

按品牌拆解这一季度，你会看到三个截然不同的故事：

The premium NIO brand delivered 62,500 vehicles in the quarter, up 69%. That’s almost entirely the All-New ES8, which NIO says just crossed 150,000 cumulative deliveries on its first anniversary. The ES9 flagship SUV added 30,000 deliveries since its launch in May.

高端品牌蔚来本季度交付62,500辆，增长69%。这几乎完全归功于全新ES8，蔚来表示该车型在上市一周年之际累计交付量刚刚突破150,000辆。ES9旗舰SUV自5月上市以来新增了30,000辆交付。

FIREFLY, the small premium brand, posted 7,327 deliveries in September, its best month of the year.

小型高端品牌FIREFLY 9月交付7,327辆，创下年内最佳月份。

ONVO is the opposite. A year ago, it was NIO’s volume engine and outsold the NIO brand in August and September 2025. In Q3, it delivered 27,728 vehicles, down 26%. Its monthly deliveries peaked at 12,029 in May, when the new L80 SUV launched, and have fallen every month since, down to 8,763 in September.

ONVO则恰恰相反。一年前，它是蔚来的销量引擎，并在2025年8月和9月超过了蔚来品牌。第三季度，它交付了27,728辆，下降26%。其月度交付量在5月新L80 SUV上市时达到峰值12,029辆，此后逐月下滑，9月降至8,763辆。

On its Q2 earnings call, management blamed ONVO’s problems on brand awareness, saying it’s roughly where NIO’s own brand was “5 to 6 years ago.” The fix it pointed to is a “major strategic new product” for ONVO in 2027. That’s a long time to wait.

在第二季度财报电话会议上，管理层将ONVO的问题归咎于品牌知名度，称其大致相当于蔚来品牌“5到6年前”的水平。它指出的解决方案是2027年为ONVO推出一款“重大战略性新产品”。这等待时间太长了。

NIO’s own Q4 goal doesn’t get it to its 2026 target

蔚来自己的Q4目标也无法达成2026年目标

Earlier this year, CEO William Li set a 2026 target of 456,000 to 489,000 deliveries, 40% to 50% growth over the 326,028 vehicles NIO delivered in 2025. After 300,301 deliveries through September, NIO needs between 155,699 and 188,699 in the fourth quarter to land in that range.

今年早些时候，CEO李斌设定了2026年交付456,000至489,000辆的目标，较蔚来2025年交付的326,028辆增长40%至50%。在截至9月交付300,301辆之后，蔚来需要在第四季度交付155,699至188,699辆才能落入该区间。

That’s 52,000 to 63,000 vehicles a month. NIO’s best month ever is 48,135, set last December.

这意味着每月52,000至63,000辆。蔚来史上最佳月份是去年12月创下的48,135辆。

而蔚来并未承诺这一目标。在第二季度电话会议上，它表示其Q4目标是“月均销量超过40,000辆”，而且即便这一目标也假设中国汽车市场会复苏。以下是各种情景的推演结果：

So NIO’s own Q4 goal lands it roughly 36,000 vehicles short of the low end of its annual target. It would also mean a fourth quarter slightly below the 124,807 it delivered in Q4 2025.

因此，蔚来自己设定的第四季度目标意味着它将比年度目标下限少约36,000辆。这也意味着第四季度将略低于2025年第四季度交付的124,807辆。

Competitive context

竞争背景

NIO isn’t the only Chinese startup losing steam, as XPeng grew just 3.7% in August. But it’s not leading either. In August, the last month with full data, NIO’s 35,836 deliveries trailed XPeng (39,107), Li Auto (37,679), and Zeekr (36,981), while Leapmotor crossed 103,129, according to the companies’ monthly reports.

蔚来并非唯一失去动力的中国初创企业，小鹏8月仅增长3.7%。但它也并非领跑者。根据各公司的月度报告，在有完整数据的最后一个月8月，蔚来交付35,836辆，落后于小鹏（39,107辆）、理想汽车（37,679辆）和极氪（36,981辆），而零跑则突破了103,129辆。

On the financial side, things are better than a year ago. NIO posted an 18.5% vehicle margin in Q2, up from 10.3%, and a positive non-GAAP operating profit. It also just sold 30% of its NIO Power battery swap unit to Geely, which brings in cash and a partner to share the swap network’s costs.

财务方面，情况比一年前要好。蔚来第二季度车辆毛利率为18.5%，高于此前的10.3%，非GAAP营业利润也实现转正。它还刚刚将NIO Power换电业务30%的股份出售给吉利，这带来了现金，也引入了一个合作伙伴来分担换电网络的成本。

Electrek’s Take

Electrek的观点

The headline number is good. NIO grew 25% in a weak Chinese market and hit its guidance. Nobody should call that a bad quarter.

标题数字是好的。在中国市场疲软的情况下，蔚来增长了25%，并达到了业绩指引。没有人应该说这是一个糟糕的季度。

But the shape of the quarter matters more than the total. NIO is now basically a two-model premium story: the ES8 and ES9 are selling, FIREFLY is growing nicely off a small base, and ONVO, the brand that was supposed to take NIO mainstream against the Model Y, is shrinking fast.

但季度的结构比总量更重要。蔚来现在基本上是一个双车型的高端故事：ES8和ES9在销售，FIREFLY在较小的基数上增长良好，而本应带领蔚来走向主流、与Model Y竞争的ONVO品牌正在快速萎缩。

And the 2026 target is gone. NIO doesn’t say so, but it would be extremely difficult to achieve without a massive reversal for the auto industry in China. To hit even the low end, it would need to beat its all-time monthly record by about 8% for three straight months. We don’t see that happening.

而2026年的目标已经无望。蔚来没有明说，但如果没有中国汽车行业的巨大逆转，这将是极其困难的。即使要达到下限，它也需要连续三个月比其历史月度纪录高出约8%。我们认为这不会发生。

That’s not a disaster. A year around 420,000 deliveries would still be close to 30% growth, with margins finally heading the right way.

这并非灾难。全年约42万辆的交付量仍将接近30%的增长，利润率也终于朝着正确的方向发展。
