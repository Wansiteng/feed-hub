Three New Apple Smart Home Products Coming on October 13

三款苹果智能家居新品将于 10 月 13 日登场


A couple of those leaked products above could be arriving very shortly, as Bloomberg's Mark Gurman says Apple will be introducing a trio of new smart home products on Tuesday, October 13.

上面泄露的其中几款产品可能很快就会亮相，因为彭博社的马克·古尔曼表示，苹果将于10 月 13 日星期二推出三款新的智能家居产品。




Apple will reportedly debut its long-rumored smart home hub product, as well as the new HomePod mini and Apple TV models, and while he didn't specify how Apple will introduce them, signs are pointing to some sort of event.

据报道，苹果将首次推出传闻已久的智能家居 hub 产品，以及新款 HomePod mini 和 Apple TV 机型，虽然他没有具体说明苹果将如何发布这些产品，但种种迹象指向某种形式的活动。



Apple retail stores have reportedly also received confidential boxes that are marked "Do Not Open Until October 8" and which are likely to contain merchandising materials. It's unclear, however, if they are for the upcoming iPhone Duo launch or something related to the smart home product introduction.

据报道，苹果零售店还收到了标有“10 月 8 日前不得打开”的机密箱子，里面很可能装有陈列物料。不过，目前尚不清楚这些是为即将到来的 iPhone Duo 发布准备的，还是与智能家居产品发布有关。



Apple Smart Home Hub to Feature iMac G4-Style Design

苹果智能家居 hub 将采用 iMac G4 风格设计


Apple's long-rumored smart home hub will have an iMac G4-style design and a host of unique features, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports.

据彭博社的马克·古尔曼报道，苹果传闻已久的智能家居 hub 将采用 iMac G4 风格的设计，并具备一系列独特功能。




The smart home hub is said to feature a square 6-inch display, with variants that can be placed on a countertop or mounted on a wall. It features a single FaceTime camera on the front, with microphones and speakers in its connected base.

据说这款智能家居 hub 配备一块方形 6 英寸显示屏，有可放置在台面上或安装在墙上的版本。它正面配有一个 FaceTime 摄像头，麦克风和扬声器则位于相连的底座中。



Leaks suggest the hub may be available in Silver, Space Gray, Starlight, and Rose Pink colors, and will be able to display user-customizable Photo Faces pulled from your photos library, memories, and shared albums, a feature that appears similar to what will be available for StandBy mode on the iPhone Duo.

泄露信息显示，这款 hub 可能有银色、深空灰色、星光色和玫瑰粉色可选，并且能够显示用户可自定义的照片表盘，内容来自你的照片图库、回忆和共享相册，这一功能似乎与 iPhone Duo 上待机模式将提供的功能类似。



iPhone Duo Reportedly Facing Production Problems Ahead of Launch

据报道 iPhone Duo 在发布前面临生产问题


The iPhone Duo is reportedly facing production difficulties ahead of its October launch, with early output expected to be limited.

据报道，iPhone Duo 在 10 月发布前面临生产困难，早期产量预计有限。




A person said to be close to Foxconn's supply chain told China's Jiemian News that final assembly yields for Apple's first foldable iPhone were only slightly above 60% as of September 17. The source said that, under Apple's current quality standards, it could take another six months to a year for the assembly line to reach a mature yield.

一位据称接近富士康供应链的人士告诉中国界面新闻，截至 9 月 17 日，苹果首款折叠屏 iPhone 的总装良率仅略高于 60%。该消息人士称，按照苹果目前的质量标准，组装线可能还需要六个月到一年才能达到成熟良率。



According to the source, Foxconn is repeatedly adjusting equipment, assembly processes, and production settings as it makes the iPhone Duo at scale. The source also said suppliers were waiting to see whether Apple would ease any quality requirements to accelerate production.

据该消息人士称，富士康在规模化生产 iPhone Duo 的过程中正反复调整设备、组装工艺和生产设置。该消息人士还表示，供应商正在观望苹果是否会放宽某些质量要求以加快生产。

Apple Planning to Launch Products Faster and More Often

苹果计划更快、更频繁地推出产品


John Ternus is marking one month as Apple CEO, and he is apparently undertaking efforts to restructure the company to accelerate product development and introduce new products more frequently, according to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman.

约翰·特努斯出任苹果 CEO 已满一个月，据彭博社的马克·古尔曼报道，他显然正在着手重组公司，以加快产品开发速度并更频繁地推出新产品。




Ternus apparently wants Apple to "broaden its range of devices and create a leaner organization with a greater focus on engineering." One proposal under consideration is to rely less on Apple's traditional spring and fall product release cycles, and introduce new devices more quickly and throughout the year. Ternus has also purportedly indicated that Apple must become "more experimental" to remain competitive in the era of artificial intelligence.

特努斯显然希望苹果“拓宽设备范围，打造一个更精简、更注重工程的组织”。正在考虑的一项提议是减少对苹果传统春秋两季产品发布周期的依赖，更快地在全年内推出新设备。据称特努斯还表示，苹果必须“更具实验精神”，才能在人工智能时代保持竞争力。
