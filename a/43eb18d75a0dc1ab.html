要点

  • 苹果首款触屏 MacBook Pro 最早 10 月发布，可能推迟到 11 月
  • 该机型正在用 macOS 27.2 beta 进行内部测试
  • 苹果或于 10 月 13 日发布新款 Apple TV 4K、HomePad 和 HomePod mini

Apple reportedly plans to launch new Home products on October 13. And according to Mark Gurman, it sounds like the rumored MacBook Pro redesign will arrive shortly after.

据报道，Apple 计划于 10 月 13 日推出新的 Home 产品。而据 Mark Gurman 称，传闻中的 MacBook Pro 重新设计似乎很快就会紧随其后登场。

Redesigned MacBook Pro with touchscreen to be introduced in October or November, per Mark Gurman

据 Mark Gurman 称，配备触控屏的重新设计版 MacBook Pro 将于 10 月或 11 月发布

Today Bloomberg launched a new weekly podcast: ‘Power On with Mark Gurman.’ In the inaugural episode, Gurman provides a launch timing update for Apple’s rumored new MacBook Pro redesign with a touchscreen, OLED, and more.

今天 Bloomberg 推出了一档全新周播播客：“Power On with Mark Gurman”。在首期节目中，Gurman 提供了 Apple 传闻中的全新 MacBook Pro 重新设计的发布时间更新，该机型将配备触控屏、OLED 等。

Here’s what he said:

以下是他所说的话：

Apple, I believe, will introduce its first touchscreen MacBook Pro this fall, as early as October. It could slip to November, but as early as October. And Apple is testing it internally using macOS 27.2, which is a beta that they’ve already put out to developers in recent weeks. So this thing is approaching.

我认为，Apple 将在今年秋季推出其首款触控屏 MacBook Pro，最早可能在 10 月。可能会推迟到 11 月，但最早是 10 月。而且 Apple 正在内部使用 macOS 27.2 对其进行测试，这是他们最近几周已经向开发者发布的测试版。所以这款产品正在临近。

Apple often launches new Macs in the late October-early November window. It sounds like that’s exactly what’s being planned for this new MacBook Pro (or MacBook Ultra) device.

Apple 经常在 10 月底至 11 月初的时间窗口发布新 Mac。听起来这正是这款新 MacBook Pro（或 MacBook Ultra）设备所计划的安排。

We’ve long been expecting and hoping for a fall launch, but Gurman previously cautioned that the product could slip into early 2027. Now that October has arrived, today’s new update is a great indication that the model is on track to launch soon.

我们一直期待并希望它能在秋季发布，但 Gurman 此前曾提醒该产品可能会推迟到 2027 年初。如今 10 月已至，今天的新消息很好地表明该机型有望很快发布。

And based on Gurman’s other reporting, it sounds like we could see several new Apple products launch within a few weeks of each other.

而根据 Gurman 的其他报道，听起来我们可能会看到多款 Apple 新产品在几周内相继发布。

A new Apple TV 4K, ‘HomePad’, and HomePod mini are rumored for October 13, with Macs following in late October or early November. And that’s all in addition to the highly anticipated iPhone Duo, which ships October 23.

传闻 新款 Apple TV 4K、“HomePad”和 HomePod mini 将于 10 月 13 日发布，Mac 则紧随其后于 10 月底或 11 月初推出。而这一切都还不包括备受期待的 iPhone Duo，该机型将于 10 月 23 日发货。
