苹果十月将迎来的 12 件新品

MacRumors · 评分 7/10：苹果十月新品前瞻，含 iPad mini、Mac Studio 等，读者想多看
原标题：12 New Things to Expect From Apple This October

要点

  • 苹果 iPhone Duo 折叠屏手机 10 月 23 日发售，10 月 16 日开启预订
  • iOS 27.1 随 iPhone Duo 出厂，iOS 27.2 将新增五种 Siri AI 语言
  • 搭载 M5 Ultra、512GB 内存的 Mac Studio 高配版 10 月底上市

October will be an especially busy month for Apple this year, with the company set to release the iPhone Duo, both iOS 27.1 and iOS 27.2, and a new Mac Studio configuration with 512GB of RAM. In addition, up to six more Apple products may be unveiled next month, such as new iPad mini, HomePod mini, and Apple TV 4K models.

对苹果来说，今年十月将格外忙碌，公司将发布 iPhone Duo、iOS 27.1 和 iOS 27.2，以及配备 512GB 内存的新款 Mac Studio 配置。此外，下个月可能还会有多达六款苹果产品亮相，例如新款 iPad mini、HomePod mini 和 Apple TV 4K 机型。




Below, we have outlined 12 new things to expect from Apple this October.

下面，我们列出了今年十月苹果值得期待的 12 件新事。



Confirmed

已确认



  • iPhone Duo: The foldable iPhone Duo launches on Friday, October 23. Pre-orders begin Friday, October 16 at 5 a.m. Pacific Time.
    iPhone Duo：可折叠的 iPhone Duo 将于 10 月 23 日星期五发售。预订于 10 月 16 日星期五太平洋时间凌晨 5 点开始。

  • iOS 27.1: Apple said the iPhone Duo will ship with iOS 27.1, so the software update is expected to be released before October 23.
    iOS 27.1：苹果表示 iPhone Duo 将预装 iOS 27.1，因此该软件更新预计会在 10 月 23 日之前发布。

  • iOS 27.2: Apple jumped ahead to an iOS 27.2 beta because iOS 27.1 is dedicated to the iPhone Duo. iOS 27.2 adds support for the five additional Siri AI languages that Apple promised would be coming in October, so accordingly we can expect iOS 27.2 to be released at some point during the month.
    iOS 27.2：苹果直接跳到了 iOS 27.2 测试版，因为 iOS 27.1 专为 iPhone Duo 打造。iOS 27.2 增加了对五种额外 Siri AI 语言的支持，苹果曾承诺这些语言将于十月推出，因此我们可以预计 iOS 27.2 会在本月某个时候发布。

  • Mac Studio (512GB RAM): While the new Mac Studio with M5 Max and M5 Ultra chips was released last week, Apple said a high-end configuration with the M5 Ultra and 512GB of RAM will be available in late October.
    Mac Studio（512GB 内存）：虽然搭载 M5 Max 和 M5 Ultra 芯片的新款 Mac Studio 已于上周发布，但苹果表示，配备 M5 Ultra 和 512GB 内存的高端配置将于十月下旬上市。



Rumored

传闻



  • iPad mini 8: OLED display, A20 Pro chip, landscape front camera, redesigned speaker system, and water-resistant design.
    iPad mini 8：OLED 显示屏、A20 Pro 芯片、横向前置摄像头、重新设计的扬声器系统以及防水设计。

  • 24" iMac: M6 chip and new color options.
    24 英寸 iMac：M6 芯片和全新配色。

  • 14" MacBook Pro: M6 chip.
    14 英寸 MacBook Pro：M6 芯片。

  • 14" and 16" MacBook Pro models with OLED displays: A major MacBook Pro redesign in late 2026 with M5 Pro and M5 Max chips, an OLED display, a touch screen, a Dynamic Island, and a thinner design.
    配备 OLED 显示屏的 14 英寸和 16 英寸 MacBook Pro 机型：MacBook Pro 将在2026 年底迎来重大重新设计，搭载M5 Pro 和 M5 Max 芯片、OLED 显示屏、触摸屏、灵动岛以及更轻薄的设计。

  • Apple TV 4K (4th Generation): A19 or A19 Pro chip, an increased 8GB to 12GB of RAM for Apple Intelligence and Siri AI support, an Apple-designed N1 chip with Wi-Fi 7 support, and an updated Siri Remote. A leak revealed the new Apple TV will look the same as the existing model.
    Apple TV 4K（第四代）：A19 或 A19 Pro 芯片、内存从 8GB 提升至 12GB 以支持 Apple Intelligence 和 Siri AI、苹果自研的 N1 芯片并支持 Wi-Fi 7，以及更新的 Siri Remote。一次泄露显示，新款 Apple TV 将与现有型号外观相同。

  • HomePod mini 2: Newer chip, Siri AI support, improved sound quality, Apple's N1 chip with Wi-Fi 7 support, a second-generation Ultra Wideband chip, and new color options, including pink and green. No major design changes.
    HomePod mini 2：更新的芯片、Siri AI 支持、改进的音质、苹果支持 Wi-Fi 7 的 N1 芯片、第二代超宽带芯片，以及包括粉色和绿色在内的新配色。设计上没有重大变化。

  • Home hub: An all-new Apple smart home hub featuring Siri AI, a 6-inch to 7-inch square display, an A18 chip or newer for Apple Intelligence, FaceTime, and more. A new operating system will feature clock faces, widgets, and more. There will be a tabletop version with a speaker base and a wall-mounted version.
    家庭中枢：一款全新的苹果智能家居中枢，搭载 Siri AI，配备 6 至 7 英寸方形显示屏，采用 A18 或更新芯片以支持 Apple Intelligence，并支持 FaceTime 等功能。全新操作系统将带来时钟表盘、小组件等特性。将推出带扬声器底座的桌面版本和壁挂版本。

  • homeOS: Apple's smart home hub is expected to run an all-new operating system that may be named homeOS, according to trademark filings.
    homeOS：据商标申请显示，苹果的智能家居中枢预计将运行一套全新的操作系统，该系统可能命名为 homeOS。
