苹果新任 CEO 可能改变手机和笔记本的发布节奏

The Verge · 评分 6/10：苹果新 CEO 拟调整产品发布节奏，属公司重大动态
原标题：Apple&#8217;s new CEO could change when it launches phones and laptops

要点

  • 苹果新 CEO 约翰·特努斯希望产品发布更频繁，不再局限于春秋两季
  • 特努斯认为苹果须更快推出产品并更具实验性，以在 AI 时代保持竞争力
  • 苹果硬件工程团队近两周裁撤多名工程项目经理，此前 Siri 和 Vision Pro 团队已裁员

John Ternus, Apple’s new CEO, wants Apple to launch products more often than its usual splashy moments in the fall and the spring, according to a Bloomberg report detailing some of Ternus’s initial plans for the company.

据彭博社的一篇报道称，苹果新任 CEO 约翰·特努斯希望苹果能比以往在秋季和春季的常规盛大发布之外更频繁地推出产品。该报道详细介绍了特努斯为公司制定的一些初步计划。

Apple regularly holds iPhone launch events in September and often announces products later in the year and in springtime, but “Ternus has indicated that Apple must introduce products more quickly and become more experimental to remain competitive in the artificial intelligence era,” Bloomberg says. The shift may not happen right away given Apple’s “long-established” development practices. Apple is rumored to have a considerable number of products in the pipeline, including a new HomePod Mini, iPad Mini, and Apple TV 4K, AirPods with cameras, touchscreen MacBooks, and a special 20th anniversary iPhone.

苹果通常会在 9 月举办 iPhone 发布会，并经常在年末和春季公布产品，但彭博社表示，“特努斯已表示，苹果必须更快地推出产品，并变得更具实验精神，才能在人工智能时代保持竞争力。”鉴于苹果“由来已久”的开发惯例，这一转变可能不会立即发生。据传苹果有相当多的产品正在筹备中，包括新的 HomePod Mini、iPad Mini 和 Apple TV 4K、带摄像头的 AirPods、触屏 MacBook，以及一款特别的 20 周年纪念版 iPhone。

Ternus is also considering reducing layers between engineers and senior executives, and the hardware engineering team dismissed “a number” of engineering program managers in the past two weeks, Bloomberg says. Those cuts follow recent layoffs on its Siri and Vision Pro teams that Bloomberg reported on in August. Apple also apparently considered laying off 5,000 AppleCare customer service staffers, but “ultimately put those plans on hold.”

彭博社称，特努斯还在考虑减少工程师与高管之间的层级，硬件工程团队在过去两周内裁掉了“若干”名工程项目经理。此前，彭博社曾在 8 月报道过苹果对Siri 和 Vision Pro 团队的裁员。苹果显然还曾考虑裁掉 5000 名 AppleCare 客服人员，但“最终搁置了这些计划”。
