乔恩·沃茨将执导下一部《星球大战》电影

Variety · 评分 6/10：大 IP 电影导演官宣，符合影视大事标准
原标题：Jon Watts to Direct Next ‘Star Wars’ Movie

要点

  • 乔恩·沃茨将执导一部正在开发中的新《星球大战》电影
  • 沃茨曾执导汤姆·霍兰德主演的《蜘蛛侠》三部曲
  • 西蒙·金伯格将负责该片剧本，他曾参与《X战警》系列

A new “Star Wars” movie is currently in development with director Jon Watts attached.

Watts is best known for directing the popular “Spider-Man” trilogy starring Tom Holland, which includes 2017’s Spider-Man: Homecoming,” 2019’s “Spider-Man: Far From Home,” and 2021’s “Spider-Man: No Way Home.” (2026’s “Spider-Man: Brand New Day” was directed by Destin Daniel Cretton.) Before being selected to direct “Spider-Man,” Watts also helmed films including “Cop Car” and “Clown.” Following his involvement with the trilogy, In 2024, it was reported that Watts, his production company Freshman Year and producer Dianne McGunigle had signed a first-look feature deal with Walt Disney Studios.

沃茨最为人熟知的是执导了由汤姆·霍兰德主演的卖座《蜘蛛侠》三部曲，包括2017年的《蜘蛛侠：英雄归来》、2019年的《蜘蛛侠：英雄远征》和2021年的《蜘蛛侠：英雄无归》。（2026年的《蜘蛛侠：崭新的一天》由德斯汀·丹尼尔·克雷顿执导。）在被选中执导《蜘蛛侠》之前，沃茨还执导过《警车》和《小丑》等影片。在三部曲之后，2024年有报道称，沃茨、他的制片公司Freshman Year以及制片人黛安·麦格尼格尔与华特迪士尼影业签署了优先看片协议。

In 2024, Watts co-created “Skeleton Crew” alongside Christopher Ford, a coming-of-age series set in the era of the New Republic that followed four young characters on a new adventure, per the show’s official logline. Jude Law starred as Jod Na Nawood, with Ravi Cabot-Conyers as Wim, Ryan Kiera Armstrong as Fern, Kyriana Kratter as KB and Robert Timothy Smith as Neel.

2024年，沃茨与克里斯托弗·福特共同创作了《骨干船员》，这部成长题材剧集设定在新共和国时代，根据官方剧情简介，讲述四位年轻角色展开全新冒险的故事。裘德·洛饰演乔德·纳·纳伍德，拉维·卡博特-康耶斯饰演维姆，瑞安·基拉·阿姆斯特朗饰演弗恩，基里亚娜·克拉特饰演KB，罗伯特·蒂莫西·史密斯饰演尼尔。

With the role, Watts joins a list of previous “Star Wars” directors that includes Irvin Kershner, Richard Marquand, J.J. Abrams, Rian Johnson and “Star Wars” creator George Lucas. Released in 2019, Abrams’ “Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker” is the most recent film in the franchise to debut theatrically.

凭借这一职位，沃茨加入了此前《星球大战》导演的行列，其中包括厄文·克什纳、理查德·马昆德、J.J.艾布拉姆斯、莱恩·约翰逊以及《星球大战》的创造者乔治·卢卡斯。2019年上映的艾布拉姆斯作品《星球大战：天行者崛起》是该系列最近一部在影院首映的电影。

As previously reported by Variety, Simon Kinberg will pen the script for the film. Kinberg is best known for being a writer, producer and director for the “X-Men” franchise, and was considered a surprising choice at the time of the announcement.

据《Variety》此前报道，西蒙·金伯格将为该片撰写剧本。金伯格最为人熟知的是担任《X战警》系列的编剧、制片人和导演，在消息公布时曾被认为是一个出人意料的选择。
