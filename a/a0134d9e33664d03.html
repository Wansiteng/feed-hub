BYD (HKEX: 1211) sold 463,561 new energy vehicles (NEVs) in September, up 16.98% year-on-year and its highest monthly total this year, extending a recovery from a weak start to 2026.

比亚迪（港交所：1211）9 月销售 463,561 辆新能源汽车（NEV），同比增长 16.98%，创下今年月度销量新高，延续了 2026 年开局疲软后的复苏势头。

The figures released by the Chinese automaker on Thursday marked its fifth consecutive month of year-on-year sales growth. Sales rose 5.28% from August, its seventh consecutive monthly increase.

这家中国车企周四公布的数据标志着其连续第五个月实现销量同比增长。销量环比 8 月增长 5.28%，为连续第七个月环比增长。

BYD sold 1,323,065 NEVs in the third quarter, up 18.75% year-on-year and 19.41% from the second quarter, according to data compiled by CnEVPost.

根据 CnEVPost 汇编的数据，比亚迪第三季度销售 1,323,065 辆 NEV，同比增长 18.75%，环比第二季度增长 19.41%。

This marked the company's return to quarterly year-on-year sales growth after 4 consecutive quarters of declines.

这标志着该公司在连续 4 个季度下滑后，重新恢复季度销量同比增长。

In the first 9 months of the year, cumulative NEV sales totaled 3,131,576 units, down 3.94% year-on-year, narrowing the decline from 6.84% in the first 8 months.

今年前 9 个月，NEV 累计销量达 3,131,576 辆，同比下降 3.94%，降幅较前 8 个月的 6.84% 有所收窄。

BYD's passenger NEV sales in September reached 456,713, up 16.19% year-on-year and 5.38% month-on-month.

比亚迪 9 月乘用车 NEV 销量达 456,713 辆，同比增长 16.19%，环比增长 5.38%。

BEVs accounted for 59.81% of passenger NEV sales in September, up from 59.12% in August and 52.17% a year earlier, showing a continued shift in the sales mix toward all-electric models.

9 月 BEV 占乘用车 NEV 销量的 59.81%，高于 8 月的 59.12% 和去年同期的 52.17%，显示销售结构持续向纯电动车型倾斜。

In the first 9 months, BYD's cumulative passenger BEV sales reached 1,629,957, up 1.50% year-on-year, reversing a 3.14% decline in the first 8 months. Passenger PHEV sales fell 10.19% to 1,448,587 over the same period.

前 9 个月，比亚迪乘用车 BEV 累计销量达 1,629,957 辆，同比增长 1.50%，扭转了前 8 个月 3.14% 的降幅。同期乘用车 PHEV 销量下降 10.19% 至 1,448,587 辆。

Overseas business remained the main driver offsetting weakness in the domestic market. BYD's overseas sales totaled 180,700 in September, up 153.59% year-on-year though down 4.63% from August.

海外业务仍是抵消国内市场疲软的主要动力。比亚迪 9 月海外销量合计 180,700 辆，同比增长 153.59%，但环比 8 月下降 4.63%。

BYD monthly China domestic sales

2024 2025 2026

In the first 9 months, BYD's overseas sales rose 92.66% to 1,342,960, while domestic sales fell 30.22% to 1,788,616.

前 9 个月，比亚迪海外销量增长 92.66% 至 1,342,960 辆，而国内销量下降 30.22% 至 1,788,616 辆。

BYD is increasing its bet in overseas markets. In a September 7 research note citing management comments, Deutsche Bank said the company had raised its 2026 overseas sales guidance to 1.9 million to 2 million vehicles and was targeting more than 2.5 million in 2027.

比亚迪正在加大海外市场的押注。德意志银行在9月7日的一份研究报告中援引管理层言论称，该公司已将2026年海外销量指引上调至190万至200万辆，并力争在2027年超过250万辆。

As sales recovered, BYD launched a major recruitment drive in September at its largest production base in Xi'an, seeking more than 8,000 workers across multiple facilities.

随着销量回升，比亚迪9月在其位于西安的最大生产基地启动了大规模招聘行动，在多个厂区招募8000多名工人。
