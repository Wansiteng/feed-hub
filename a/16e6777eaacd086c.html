苹果称 iPhone Duo 折叠屏有可更换层以减少折痕

原标题：Apple says iPhone Duo has ‘replaceable’ folding display layer to minimize crease

要点

  • 苹果称 iPhone Duo 折叠屏采用可更换盖层以减轻折痕
  • AppleCare 下更换盖层收费 19 美元，无保险价格未知
  • 硬件工程副总裁 Kate Bergeron 称哑光涂层有助淡化折痕

Foldable displays tend to show a more visible crease over time, but Apple‘s Kate Bergeron says iPhone Duo has a replaceable cover layer on its folding screen to help mitigate that issue.

可折叠显示屏往往会随着时间推移出现更明显的折痕，但 Apple 的 Kate Bergeron 表示，iPhone Duo 的折叠屏上有一层可更换的覆盖层，有助于缓解这一问题。

iPhone Duo ‘cover layer’ on folding display can be replaced over time for a day-one look

iPhone Duo 折叠屏的“覆盖层”可随时间更换，保持如新

After the iPhone Duo was introduced last month, Apple execs gave interviews explaining how the company very intentionally sought to maximize durability and minimize crease issues that plague other foldables.

iPhone Duo 于上个月发布后，Apple 高管接受了采访，解释公司如何刻意追求最大化耐用性，并尽量减少困扰其他折叠屏的折痕问题。

As part of that effort, it seems the iPhone Duo has a unique design feature to ensure a “day one” type experience for years to come: a replaceable cover layer on the folding display.

作为这一努力的一部分，iPhone Duo 似乎拥有一项独特的设计特性，以确保未来多年都能保持“如新”般的体验：折叠屏上可更换的覆盖层。

Here’s Kate Bergeron, Apple VP of Hardware Engineering, speaking to @angietutorials on TikTok:

以下是 Apple 硬件工程副总裁 Kate Bergeron 在 TikTok 上对 @angietutorials 所说的话：

“The matte display coating does a really nice job of, I’ll say minimizing the crease. But there’s a cover layer on top, and we’ve done a significant amount of engineering work to help that wear in as long of a manner as we can, and to make sure that you get the most out of that layer’s performance. It’s actually replaceable, which is a really nice thing for the customer, that you can peel off that layer and get a new one.”

“哑光显示屏涂层在减少折痕方面做得非常好。但上面还有一层覆盖层，我们做了大量的工程工作，尽可能延长它的耐用性，并确保你能充分发挥这一层的性能。它实际上是可以更换的，这对客户来说非常棒，你可以把这层撕下来换一个新的。”

Apple refers to this “cover layer” in its list of AppleCare estimated service costs for iPhone Duo. As spotted by Quinn Nelson, a cover layer replacement costs $19 with AppleCare. Its cost without coverage is currently unknown.

Apple 在 iPhone Duo 的 AppleCare 预估服务费用列表中提到了这个“覆盖层”。据 Quinn Nelson 发现，在 AppleCare 下更换覆盖层费用为 19 美元。没有保障时的费用目前未知。

So if you’re worried about spending $2,000+ on an iPhone Duo only to find the display crease become more prominent over time, it sounds like Apple has a solution.

所以，如果你担心花 2000 多美元买一台 iPhone Duo，结果发现屏幕折痕随时间变得越来越明显，听起来 Apple 已经有了解决方案。
