要点

  • 中国工信部或允许阿里巴巴、字节跳动购买英伟达新款 RTX Pro 5500 芯片
  • 工信部要求部分企业上报采购数量及用途，并告知拟批准购买

The Chinese government has signaled it may allow companies such as Alibaba Group Holding Ltd. and ByteDance Ltd to purchase Nvidia Corp.’s new RTX Pro 5500 chips, The Information reported, citing people familiar with the matter.

据 The Information 援引知情人士报道，中国政府已释放信号，可能允许阿里巴巴集团和字节跳动等企业采购英伟达新款 RTX Pro 5500 芯片。

The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology recently asked some companies to report on their plans to purchase the high-end graphics processor chip, which was released this month, including how many of them and what they would use the product for, the report said, adding that the ministry told some of the firms it intends to approve the purchases.

报道称，工业和信息化部近期要求部分企业上报这款本月发布的高端图形处理器芯片的采购计划，包括采购数量及用途，并告知其中一些企业其打算批准相关采购。
