档期提醒：Connie Britton 的 Hulu 新剧、《权力的游戏》电影等

Hulu has released the trailer for "Phony," a half-hour mystery series starring "The White Lotus" vets Connie Britton and Sam Nivola. It premieres Tuesday, October 20, with two episodes, then one episode weekly.

Hulu 发布了《Phony》的预告片，这是一部半小时悬疑剧，由《白莲花度假村》老将 Connie Britton 和 Sam Nivola 主演。该剧将于 10 月 20 日星期二首播，连播两集，之后每周一集。

"Phony" follows "a teenager who wakes up in the hospital after a car accident to discover his mom — his only parent and his best friend — appears to have been replaced by an impostor," according to the official logline. Meera Syal, Grace Brennan, Mahaela Park, Maliq Johnson, and Colter Lee also star.

根据官方剧情简介，《Phony》讲述“一名少年在车祸后于医院醒来，发现他的母亲——他唯一的亲人和最好的朋友——似乎被一个冒名顶替者取代了”。Meera Syal、Grace Brennan、Mahaela Park、Maliq Johnson 和 Colter Lee 也参演。

Nick Paley wrote and directed the first episode, and executive-produces alongside co-showrunner Anthony King ("Jury Duty Presents: Company Retreat"), Britton, Charlie Alderman, Drew Goddard, and Sarah Esberg. Watch a sneak peek above.

Nick Paley 编写并执导了第一集，并与联合剧集主管 Anthony King（《Jury Duty Presents: Company Retreat》）、Connie Britton、Charlie Alderman、Drew Goddard 和 Sarah Esberg 共同担任执行制片人。请观看上方的抢先片段。

In other scheduling news...

其他档期消息……

  • The theatrical film "Game of Thrones: Aegon's Conquest" — written by Beau Willimon, directed by Owen Harris ("A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms"), and reportedly centered on Targaryen dynasty founder Aegon I — will be released June 6, 2029, per Variety.
    据 Variety 报道，院线电影《权力的游戏：Aegon's Conquest》——由 Beau Willimon 编剧、Owen Harris（《七国骑士》）执导，据称聚焦坦格利安王朝创始人伊耿一世——将于 2029 年 6 月 6 日上映。
  • "Vanderpump Rules: Lisa Las Vegas" premieres Tuesday, October 20 at 9/8c on Bravo with two episodes, followed by the third and final episode Wednesday, October 21 at 9 p.m. The three-part series follows Lisa Vanderpump as she prepares to open her namesake Las Vegas hotel. Watch a sneak peek:
    《Vanderpump Rules: Lisa Las Vegas》将于 10 月 20 日星期二晚 9 点（东部时间 8 点）在 Bravo 首播，连播两集，随后于 10 月 21 日星期三晚 9 点播出第三集也是最后一集。这部三集剧集跟随 Lisa Vanderpump，记录她筹备开设以自己名字命名的拉斯维加斯酒店的过程。请观看抢先片段：
  • Netflix has unveiled the trailer for "The New Stanford Prison Experiment," a six-episode series premiering Wednesday, October 21 that puts 30 willing participants inside a real prison, where they are randomly assigned as guards or prisoners. "Thrust into a system of power, hierarchy, and control, they'll discover whether ordinary people inevitably conform to the roles they're given — or whether empathy, morality, and individuality can withstand the forces pushing them in the opposite direction," according to the official logline.
    Netflix 发布了《The New Stanford Prison Experiment》的预告片，这部六集剧集将于 10 月 21 日星期三首播，将 30 名自愿参与者置于一座真实监狱中，他们被随机分配为狱警或囚犯。根据官方剧情简介，“被推入一个充满权力、等级和控制的体系后，他们将发现普通人是否会不可避免地顺从于被赋予的角色——或者同理心、道德和个性是否能够抵挡住将他们推向相反方向的力量。”
  • "The One About Matthew Perry," a three-part docuseries about the late "Friends" star, premieres Tuesday, October 27 on Netflix. Talking heads featured in the trailer include former NBC Entertainment president Warren Littlefield and Perry's sister, Mia Perry Bowick.
    关于已故《老友记》明星 Matthew Perry 的三集纪录片《The One About Matthew Perry》将于 10 月 27 日星期二在 Netflix 首播。预告片中出现的受访者包括前 NBC 娱乐总裁 Warren Littlefield 和 Perry 的妹妹 Mia Perry Bowick。
  • "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County" premieres Thursday, November 12 on Hulu. The spin-off follows "a new group of dynamic young mothers" whose differing beliefs put them at odds as they come together to build their answer to #MomTok, according to the official synopsis. The cast includes Ashleigh Pease, Aspyn Ovard, Avery Woods, Bobbi Althoff, Jen Affleck, Jordan McClanahan, Madison Bontempo, McCall DaPron, and Salomé Andrea.
    《The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County》将于 11 月 12 日星期四在 Hulu 首播。根据官方简介，这部衍生剧跟随“一群充满活力的新晋年轻母亲”，她们因信仰不同而产生分歧，却又聚在一起打造她们对 #MomTok 的回应。演员阵容包括 Ashleigh Pease、Aspyn Ovard、Avery Woods、Bobbi Althoff、Jen Affleck、Jordan McClanahan、Madison Bontempo、McCall DaPron 和 Salomé Andrea。
