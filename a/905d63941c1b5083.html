新款 Apple TV 4K、HomeView 和 HomePod mini：刚泄露的 16 项细节

MacRumors · 评分 7/10：苹果新品细节汇总，含 A18 芯片等实质信息
原标题：New Apple TV 4K, HomeView, and HomePod Mini: 16 Details Just Leaked

要点

  • 苹果智能家居中枢命名为 HomeView，搭载 A18 芯片，支持 Siri AI 语音控制
  • HomeView 有桌面版和壁挂版，桌面版配金属音箱底座，音质优于 HomePod mini
  • HomeView 10 月 13 日发布，约 11 月中旬上市，无第三方 App Store

Just two days before Apple's "Welcome home" experience in New York, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman has leaked several details about the new products set to be unveiled, including an all-new smart home hub, a HomePod mini 2, and a new Apple TV 4K.

就在苹果于纽约举办“Welcome home”体验活动的前两天，彭博社的马克·古尔曼爆料了即将发布的新品多项细节，包括一款全新的智能家居中枢、HomePod mini 2以及新款Apple TV 4K。




Here is an all-in-one summary of the details shared by Gurman:

以下是古尔曼所分享细节的汇总：

  • Apple's home hub with a touch-enabled display will be named HomeView, seemingly confirming a trademark filing discovered by MacRumors.
    苹果带触控屏的智能家居中枢将被命名为HomeView，这似乎证实了MacRumors发现的一份商标申请。

  • HomeView is equipped with an A18 chip, which has 8GB of RAM in iPhones.
    HomeView搭载A18芯片，该芯片在iPhone上配备8GB内存。

  • HomeView will support Siri AI for voice control.
    HomeView将支持Siri AI进行语音控制。

  • HomeView will be available in a tabletop version with a speaker base made of metal, as well as a wall-mounted version with a flat, square wall plate.
    HomeView将提供桌面版本，配备金属材质的扬声器底座，以及壁挂版本，配有扁平的方形壁挂板。

  • The tabletop HomeView will offer "better sound quality" than the HomePod mini.
    桌面版HomeView将提供比HomePod mini“更好的音质”。

  • The tabletop HomeView will be available in four finishes: Gray, Silver, Starlight, and Rose. The wall-mounted version will be limited to Gray and Silver.
    桌面版HomeView将有四种配色：灰色、银色、星光色和玫瑰色。壁挂版本将仅限灰色和银色。

  • HomeView will be unveiled this Tuesday, October 13, but the device is apparently not launching until "around mid-November."
    HomeView将于本周二，即10月13日亮相，但该设备显然要到“11月中旬左右”才会正式发售。

  • HomeView will have a selection of Apple apps, but no App Store for third-party apps.
    HomeView将预装一系列苹果应用，但没有供第三方应用使用的App Store。

  • HomeView will be accompanied by an "Apple Home Collection" of accessories that brands such as LG and Schneider Electric developed in partnership with Apple. This will include a smart doorbell, deadbolt lock, thermostat, temperature sensor, indoor and outdoor security cameras, and a floodlight camera.
    HomeView将配套推出“Apple Home Collection”配件系列，由LG和施耐德电气等品牌与苹果合作开发。其中包括智能门铃、防盗锁、恒温器、温度传感器、室内外安全摄像头以及泛光灯摄像头。

  • LG's accessories for the HomeView will not ship "until later this year at the earliest."
    LG为HomeView打造的配件“最早也要到今年晚些时候”才会出货。

  • The new HomePod mini will be equipped with the Apple Watch Series 10's S10 chip, up from the S5 chip in the current model.
    新款HomePod mini将搭载Apple Watch Series 10的S10芯片，较当前型号的S5芯片有所升级。

  • The new HomePod mini will be available in green, pink, blue, white, and midnight.
    新款HomePod mini将提供绿色、粉色、蓝色、白色和午夜色。

  • The new Apple TV 4K will be equipped with an A19 chip, which has 8GB of RAM.
    新款Apple TV 4K将搭载A19芯片，配备8GB内存。

  • The new Apple TV 4K may have built-in microphones for Siri AI.
    新款Apple TV 4K可能内置麦克风，以支持Siri AI。

  • The new Apple TV 4K may have an updated Siri Remote with a built-in speaker that makes it easier to find.
    新款Apple TV 4K可能配备更新版Siri Remote，内置扬声器以便于寻找。

  • The new HomePod mini and Apple TV 4K models will be unveiled on October 13 and launch on Friday, October 30.
    新款HomePod mini和Apple TV 4K机型将于10月13日亮相，并于10月30日星期五发售。
