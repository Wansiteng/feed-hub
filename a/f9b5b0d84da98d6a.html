MacBook Pro 重新设计即将到来，带来我们等待多年的三项功能

9to5Mac · 评分 6/10：MacBook Pro 大改款传闻，含三项新功能，读者关注 Apple Silicon
原标题：MacBook Pro redesign is coming, with three features we’ve waited years for

要点

  • 苹果计划10月发布重新设计的MacBook Pro，可能命名为MacBook Ultra
  • 新款MacBook Pro将首次配备OLED屏幕，带来更纯黑、更高对比度并改善续航
  • 新款MacBook Pro将首次支持触控屏，但触控只是辅助输入方式

Apple has a big MacBook Pro redesign in the works (possibly called MacBook Ultra) that’s expected to launch in October. Here are three rumored new features that we’ve waited a long time for.

Apple 正在筹备一次重大的 MacBook Pro 重新设计（可能命名为 MacBook Ultra），预计将于 10 月发布。以下是三项目前传闻中的新功能，我们已经等待了很久。

#1: OLED display

#1：OLED 显示屏

It’s hard to overstate the value of display technology for a product that many users spend all day looking at. Yet for years, the Mac has lagged behind Apple’s other products in offering the most cutting-edge display tech.

对于许多用户整天盯着看的产品来说，显示技术的价值怎么强调都不为过。然而多年来，Mac 在提供最前沿显示技术方面一直落后于 Apple 的其他产品。

OLED has been a mainstay on iPhone for years, and iPad Pro introduced a tandem OLED in 2024 that many Mac users pined for. Now with the new MacBook Pro, OLED is finally coming to Mac.

OLED 多年来一直是 iPhone 的标配，iPad Pro 在 2024 年引入了串联 OLED，令许多 Mac 用户羡慕不已。如今随着新款 MacBook Pro 的到来，OLED 终于要登陆 Mac 了。

The redesigned MacBook Pro with OLED is expected to offer truer blacks, improved contrast, and likely help out with battery life too. Here’s hoping it’s able to match the impressive tandem OLED the iPad offers.

采用 OLED 的重新设计版 MacBook Pro 预计将带来更纯正的黑色、更高的对比度，并且很可能有助于提升电池续航。希望它能够媲美 iPad 上令人印象深刻的串联 OLED。

#2: Touchscreen

#2：触控屏

Apple has long argued that the Mac doesn’t need a touchscreen, despite competing products nearly all offering touch. But rumors indicate we’re finally getting the first Mac with touch support.

Apple 长期以来一直主张 Mac 不需要触控屏，尽管竞品几乎都提供了触控功能。但传闻表明，我们终于要迎来首款支持触控的 Mac 了。

The new MacBook Pro will have a touchscreen, and macOS 27 is already packed with hints that such a feature is coming. We’re expecting further software optimizations to make touch feel like a natural experience on the Mac.

新款 MacBook Pro 将配备触控屏，而 macOS 27 中已经充满了暗示这一功能即将到来。我们预计 Apple 会进一步优化软件，让触控在 Mac 上成为一种自然的体验。

Despite touch being available, macOS will remain tailored primarily for trackpad and keyboard input. Touch will be a secondary tool on the Mac, not replacing traditional input methods but rather supplementing them for users who have grown accustomed to touchscreens on iPhones, iPads, and PCs.

尽管支持触控，macOS 仍将主要针对触控板和键盘输入进行定制。触控将成为 Mac 上的辅助工具，不会取代传统输入方式，而是为那些已经习惯在 iPhone、iPad 和 PC 上使用触控屏的用户提供补充。

#3: No more notch

#3：告别刘海

When Apple last redesigned the MacBook Pro in 2021, it introduced a notch design that first came to iPhone. The notch then spread to the MacBook Air too, making it a fixture of modern Mac design.

Apple 上一次重新设计 MacBook Pro 是在 2021 年，当时引入了最早出现在 iPhone 上的刘海设计。随后刘海也扩展到了 MacBook Air，成为现代 Mac 设计的固定元素。

But the new MacBook Pro will reportedly drop the notch in favor of a hole-punch camera cutout that likely resembles what we’ve seen on iPhone Duo.

但据报道，新款 MacBook Pro 将取消刘海，改用打孔摄像头切口，外观可能类似于我们在 iPhone Duo 上看到的设计。

The Dynamic Island will be brought over to macOS too, making the camera cutout integrate seamlessly on the Mac like it does on iPhone. As someone who finds the notch more of an eyesore on the Mac than I ever did on iPhone, I’m excited to see the notch disappear soon.

灵动岛也将被引入 macOS，使摄像头切口在 Mac 上像在 iPhone 上一样无缝融合。作为一个觉得 Mac 上的刘海比 iPhone 上更碍眼的人，我很期待看到刘海很快消失。
