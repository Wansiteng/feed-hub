Microsoft just wrapped up a big Windows and Surface-focused keynote in San Francisco.

微软刚刚在旧金山结束了一场以 Windows 和 Surface 为主题的大型发布会。

The biggest announcement was arguably the release details about the Surface Laptop Ultra, its new laptop that’s powered by Nvidia’s RTX Spark Arm-based chip. The machine will start at $2,599 for a configuration with an 8-core CPU, 24GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage, and it will be released on October 16th. The laptop also has built-in magnetic USB-C charging instead of the proprietary Surface Connect magnetic charging port. The Verge‘s Tom Warren published a deep dive all about the new laptop. Microsoft also announced that the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, a mini PC for developers, is available for preorder for $5,999 ahead of shipping in November.

其中最重要的消息大概要数 Surface Laptop Ultra 的发布细节，这是其搭载 Nvidia RTX Spark 基于 Arm 架构芯片的新款笔记本。该机型起售价为 2,599 美元，配置为 8 核 CPU、24GB 内存和 512GB 存储，将于 10 月 16 日发售。这款笔记本还配备了内置磁吸式 USB-C 充电，取代了专有的 Surface Connect 磁吸充电接口。The Verge 的 Tom Warren 发布了一篇关于这款新笔记本的深度解析。微软还宣布，面向开发者的迷你主机 Surface RTX Spark Dev Box 已开启预售，售价 5,999 美元，将于 11 月发货。

In addition, Microsoft shared details about some updates coming to Windows, including “Hybrid Intelligence” features that lets Copilot use files on your Windows PC to take actions and a new search experience that lets you do quick actions from the search bar.

此外，微软还分享了Windows 即将推出的一些更新的细节，包括“Hybrid Intelligence”功能，可让 Copilot 利用你 Windows PC 上的文件来执行操作，以及全新的搜索体验，让你可以直接从搜索栏执行快捷操作。
