小米在中国正式发布 Sky Nomad 系列

electrive · 评分 6/10：小米首推增程车型，新能源车产品线重要扩展
原标题：Xiaomi officially launches Sky Nomad series in China

要点

  • 小米汽车发布 Sky Nomad 系列，首次进入增程式电动车市场
  • N70 Pro 起售价 20.99 万元，N70 Max 23.99 万元，N90 Max 26.99 万元
  • 全系搭载 1.5T 增程器，综合续航最高 1705 公里

Xiaomi Auto has unveiled its new Sky Nomad series in China, marking the company’s first entry into the extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) segment. Previously, its lineup consisted solely of the all-electric SU7 sedan and YU7 SUV.

小米汽车在中国发布了全新的 Sky Nomad 系列，标志着该公司首次进军增程式电动汽车（EREV）细分市场。此前，其产品线仅包括纯电动的 SU7 轿车和 YU7 SUV。

At launch, the new series includes the five-seater N70 Pro and N70 Max, as well as the seven-seater N90 Max. There is also a five-seater Explorer version of the N90 Max, featuring an electrically retractable roof for camping applications. Pricing starts at 209,900 yuan (~€27,600 euros) for the N70 Pro. The N70 Max starts at 239,900 yuan (~€31,500), the N90 Max at 269,900 yuan (~€35,400), and the Explorer version at 299,900 yuan (~€39,400). Notably, the N70 Max is priced below its previously announced pre-order figure.

上市之初，新系列包括五座版 N70 Pro 和 N70 Max，以及七座版 N90 Max。此外还有五座版 N90 Max Explorer，配备电动可伸缩车顶，适用于露营场景。N70 Pro 起售价为 209,900 元（约合 27,600 欧元）。N70 Max 起售价 239,900 元（约合 31,500 欧元），N90 Max 起售价 269,900 元（约合 35,400 欧元），Explorer 版起售价 299,900 元（约合 39,400 欧元）。值得注意的是，N70 Max 的定价低于此前公布的预售价。

The N70 is positioned as a mid-size to large five-seater SUV and measures 4.96 metres in length. The base N70 Pro features front-wheel drive and a 210 kW electric motor. Its 52 kWh LFP battery is said to offer a CLTC electric range of 351 km. With the range extender, Xiaomi claims a total range of up to 1,351 km.

N70 定位为中大型五座 SUV，车长 4.96 米。入门版 N70 Pro 采用前轮驱动，搭载 210 kW 电机。其 52 kWh LFP 电池据称可提供 351 km 的 CLTC 纯电续航。加上增程器后，小米宣称综合续航最高可达 1,351 km。

The N70 Max features all-wheel drive with dual electric motors and a system output of 310 kW. Its 76 kWh battery, using ternary cell chemistry, is said to deliver up to 505 km of electric CLTC range, with a combined range of 1,461 km. According to Xiaomi, up to 310 km of range can be replenished in 15 minutes.

N70 Max 采用双电机四轮驱动，系统输出功率为 310 kW。其 76 kWh 三元锂电池据称可提供最高 505 km 的 CLTC 纯电续航，综合续航为 1,461 km。据小米称，15 分钟可补充最高 310 km 的续航。

The larger N90 Max measures 5.29 metres and offers seven seats in a 2+2+3 configuration. It also uses a 76 kWh battery and a 310 kW all-wheel-drive system. The electric CLTC range is stated as 464 km, with a combined range of up to 1,705 km.

尺寸更大的 N90 Max 车长 5.29 米，采用 2+2+3 的七座布局。它同样搭载 76 kWh 电池和 310 kW 四驱系统。CLTC 纯电续航标称为 464 km，综合续航最高可达 1,705 km。

The Explorer version, on the other hand, has five seats and a factory-integrated electric pop-up roof. The rear seats can be folded into a sleeping area, and Xiaomi also highlights features such as underfloor heating. The electric range is 462 km, with a combined range of 1,688 km.

Explorer 版则采用五座布局，并配备原厂集成电动升降车顶。后排座椅可折叠成睡眠区，小米还重点介绍了地暖等功能。纯电续航为 462 km，综合续航为 1,688 km。

All variants use a 1.5-litre turbocharged petrol engine as a range extender. According to Xiaomi, the battery in each model can be charged from 20 to 80 per cent in around 18 minutes.

所有版本均搭载 1.5 升涡轮增压汽油发动机作为增程器。据小米称，各车型的电池均可在约 18 分钟内从 20% 充至 80%。

For the new series, Xiaomi has also announced the so-called Dragonscale battery. Developed in collaboration with CALB and Sunwoda, this will be used in the N70 and N90 models. Xiaomi highlights features such as its own cloud-based battery management system, additional high-voltage protection mechanisms, and enhanced thermal safety.

针对新系列，小米还发布了所谓的 Dragonscale 电池。该电池由小米与中创新航和欣旺达联合开发，将用于 N70 和 N90 车型。小米重点介绍了其自有的云端电池管理系统、额外的高压保护机制以及增强的热安全性能。

The manufacturer also links the battery to a comprehensive safety guarantee. For non-commercial vehicles, Xiaomi promises the first owners lifetime protection against spontaneous combustion due to quality defects, as well as against battery fires following collisions or underbody damage. In the event of spontaneous combustion caused by a quality defect, Xiaomi claims it will even provide a replacement vehicle with equivalent specifications.

厂商还将该电池与全面的安全保障相挂钩。对于非营运车辆，小米承诺为首任车主提供因质量缺陷导致自燃的终身保障，以及碰撞或底盘受损后电池起火的终身保障。若因质量缺陷导致自燃，小米声称甚至将提供同等规格的替代车辆。

Xiaomi also prioritises advanced driver assistance and infotainment as standard equipment. All Sky Nomad models feature Xiaomi HAD based on the Nvidia Drive Thor with 700 TOPS of computing power. The N70 Pro and N70 Max each have one LiDAR sensor, while the N90 Max and Explorer have two. Additional features include 4D millimetre-wave radar and HyperOS based on the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3.

小米还将高级驾驶辅助和信息娱乐系统作为标准配置优先提供。所有 Sky Nomad 车型均搭载基于 Nvidia Drive Thor 的 Xiaomi HAD，算力达 700 TOPS。N70 Pro 和 N70 Max 各配备一颗 LiDAR 传感器，而 N90 Max 和 Explorer 则配备两颗。其他功能还包括 4D 毫米波雷达以及基于 Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 的 HyperOS。

The new series is also significant for Xiaomi’s delivery targets. The company aims to deliver a total of 550,000 vehicles in 2026. In the first seven months, 216,322 cars were delivered, representing a 14.8 per cent increase year-over-year. Company CEO Lei Jun also stated that Xiaomi Auto has now delivered a cumulative total of more than 800,000 vehicles.

新系列对小米的交付目标也意义重大。公司计划在2026年共交付55万辆汽车。前七个月已交付216,322辆，同比增长14.8%。公司CEO雷军还表示，小米汽车累计交付量现已超过80万辆。
