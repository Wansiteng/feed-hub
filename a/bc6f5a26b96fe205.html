DeepSeek has publicly released software it created with Huawei Technologies Co. to program AI chips, showcasing its close partnership with the Chinese company working to develop technologies to replace Nvidia Corp.

DeepSeek公开了其与华为合作开发的AI芯片编程软件，展现出其与这家正致力于研发替代英伟达技术的中国公司的紧密合作关系。

The AI lab is making its software toolkit for Huawei’s Ascend accelerators open-source and free to download, the startup said on its WeChat account Wednesday. Those tools include TileLang, China’s answer to Nvidia’s CUDA: a software platform considered the global standard for developing AI.

这家AI实验室将其面向华为Ascend加速器的软件工具包开源并免费提供下载，该初创公司周三在其微信公众号上表示。这些工具包括TileLang——中国对标英伟达CUDA的产物：CUDA是被视为全球AI开发标准的软件平台。
