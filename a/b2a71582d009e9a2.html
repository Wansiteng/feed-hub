彭博：配备 OLED 屏的新 iPad mini 将比现款贵不少

9to5Mac · 评分 6/10：iPad mini 新品价格实质信息，读者关注苹果硬件
原标题：Bloomberg: New iPad mini with OLED display will be significantly more expensive than current model

要点

  • 新一代 iPad mini 预计本月晚些时候发布，起售价可能涨至 799 美元左右
  • 现款 iPad mini 起售价 599 美元，涨价后比 11 英寸 iPad Air 还贵
  • 新款 iPad mini 将首次采用 OLED 屏幕，并升级扬声器和芯片

The current iPad mini sells for $599 in its starting 128 GB configuration. Apple is set to introduce the next-generation iPad mini later this month and — while the starting storage may increase — interested buyers should start probably bracing for a price rise.

现款 iPad mini 的起步配置为 128 GB，售价 599 美元。Apple 将于本月晚些时候推出下一代 iPad mini，尽管起步存储容量可能提升，但有意购买的用户恐怕要开始做好涨价的心理准备了。

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman says he is hearing that the new iPad mini will be “considerably more expensive”, floating a new price closer to the $799 mark.

Bloomberg 的 Mark Gurman 表示，他听说新款 iPad mini 将“贵得多”，并给出了接近 799 美元的新价格预期。

If that turned out to be the case, it would represent a $200 jump compared to the current iPad mini baseline, and slot it above the 11-inch iPad Air starting price in the current lineup.

如果情况属实，这意味着相比现款 iPad mini 的起步价上涨 200 美元，其定价也将高于当前产品线中 11 英寸 iPad Air 的起售价。

Of course, Apple will argue that you are getting more product for that money. The new iPad mini is expected to feature an OLED display for the first time, with a new chassis design including thinner bezels.

当然，Apple 会辩称这笔钱能换来更多东西。新款 iPad mini 预计将首次采用 OLED 显示屏，并配备全新机身设计，边框更窄。

The new iPad mini is also getting upgraded speaker system and a much more powerful chip upgrade. We believe it will be powered by the A20 Pro chip, the same chip as found in Apple’s high-end iPhone 18 Pro models.

新款 iPad mini 还将升级扬声器系统，并搭载性能大幅提升的芯片。我们认为它将采用 A20 Pro 芯片，与 Apple 高端 iPhone 18 Pro 机型所用的芯片相同。

However, it remains unclear whether the OLED display in the new iPad mini will be technically comparable to the iPad Pro’s industry-leading tandem OLED panel. There have also been conflicting reports about whether it will support 120Hz ProMotion.

不过，目前尚不清楚新款 iPad mini 的 OLED 显示屏在技术上能否与 iPad Pro 业界领先的双层串联 OLED 面板相媲美。此外，关于它是否支持 120Hz ProMotion，各方报道也存在矛盾。

The new iPad mini should be announced at a media event later this month, alongside new OLED MacBook Pros. But first, on Tuesday, Apple will host a ‘Welcome home’ event where it will unveil a new smart home strategy, including its first smart home display product.

新款 iPad mini 预计将在本月晚些时候的一场媒体活动上发布，同场亮相的还有新款 OLED MacBook Pro。但在此之前，Apple 将于周二举办一场“Welcome home”活动，公布全新的智能家居战略，其中包括其首款智能家居显示屏产品。
