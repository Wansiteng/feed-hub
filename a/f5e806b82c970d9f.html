微软发布搭载英伟达芯片的新 AI PC 与改版 Windows 11

TechCrunch · AI · 评分 6/10：AI PC 新品，可运行模型与 agent，硬件相关
原标题：Microsoft releases new Nvidia-chip AI PCs with revamped Windows 11

要点

  • 微软发布 Surface Laptop Ultra，起售价 2600 美元，顶配 5900 美元
  • 微软推出 Surface RTX Spark Dev Box 工作站，起售价 6000 美元
  • 新设备搭载 RTX Spark 芯片，可本地免费运行 AI 模型

Back in June, Nvidia announced that it had secured agreements with Microsoft and a host of other PC makers to develop AI- and agent-ready PCs for Windows based on its RTX Spark chip, but details on the machines were scant at the time.

早在今年6月，Nvidia 宣布已与Microsoft及其他多家PC厂商达成协议，基于其RTX Spark芯片开发面向Windows的AI和agent就绪PC，但当时关于这些机器的细节寥寥无几。

On Wednesday, at an event held in San Francisco during the city’s Tech Week, Microsoft revealed the specs and price for its new Surface Laptop Ultra. The PC comes in two base models, one that starts at $2,600 and one with a more powerful chip that starts at $3,700. Prices rise from there with options for more memory and storage, up to $5,900. (Microsoft says the highest-end device is already out of stock.)

周三，在旧金山Tech Week期间举办的一场活动上，Microsoft公布了其新款Surface Laptop Ultra的规格和价格。这款PC有两个基础型号，一款起售价2,600美元，另一款搭载更强大芯片的起售价3,700美元。在此基础上，随着内存和存储配置的提升，价格最高可达5,900美元。（Microsoft表示，最高端机型已经售罄。）

Microsoft also revealed its new workstation powered by the RTX Spark chip: the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, which starts priced at $6,000 and comes equipped with Microsoft dev tools like VS Code, GitHub Copilot CLI, WSL, and PowerShell 7.

Microsoft还公布了其搭载RTX Spark芯片的新款工作站：Surface RTX Spark Dev Box，起售价6,000美元，预装了VS Code、GitHub Copilot CLI、WSL和PowerShell 7等Microsoft开发工具。

The pitch for both this PC and workstation is that they are designed to run AI models on-device locally (and for free) with the CPU, GPU, unified memory, and other hardware modifications, like superior cooling, necessary to handle them.

这款PC和工作站的卖点在于，它们专为在本地设备上（免费）运行AI模型而设计，配备了CPU、GPU、统一内存以及其他必要的硬件改造，比如更强的散热能力，以应对这些模型的需求。

Plus, they are running a revamped version of Windows 11 that includes a feature called “Execution Containers.” This makes it easier to sandbox AI agents, the company says. That feature will be available for all Windows 11 users, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said at the event.

此外，它们运行的是经过大幅改进的Windows 11，其中包含一项名为“Execution Containers”的功能。该公司称，这让AI agent的沙箱隔离变得更加容易。Microsoft CEO萨提亚·纳德拉在活动上表示，该功能将面向所有Windows 11用户开放。

Nadella explained: “One of the things we realized in the last 3-4 years is that just having a model doesn’t do much for anything. You really do need to orchestrate, and you need to have memory outside of the model. You need to have this harnessed layer that is able to take multiple models plus context, plus memory, and the action space.”

纳德拉解释道：“过去三四年我们意识到的一件事是，光有一个模型做不了什么。你确实需要编排，需要在模型之外有记忆。你需要这样一个被驾驭的层，能够整合多个模型，加上上下文、记忆以及行动空间。”

He added that Microsoft is enabling that “not just for our apps but apps for anyone’s agent. That’s what the Windows platform is all about going forward.”

他补充说，Microsoft正在让这一点“不仅适用于我们自己的应用，也适用于任何人的agent应用。这就是Windows平台未来的方向。”

The devices are clearly aimed at developers, so much so that Microsoft is offering up to $1,000 off to people who trade in a MacBook Pro, as Macs have become a popular choice for developers and AI agent enthusiasts, particularly in Silicon Valley.

这些设备显然面向开发者，以至于Microsoft向以MacBook Pro折价换购的用户提供最高1,000美元的优惠，因为Mac已成为开发者和AI agent爱好者的热门选择，尤其是在硅谷。

Still, these Surface PCs, with such powerful graphics processing, are also capable of content creation, video processing, and gaming, the company promises.

不过，该公司承诺，这些拥有如此强大图形处理能力的Surface PC同样能够胜任内容创作、视频处理和游戏。

Microsoft wasn’t the only PC company to announce the specs for its new Nvidia-RTX Spark AI Windows PCs on Wednesday. Dell also announced the details of its previously announced Dell XPS 16 Creator Edition PC. It’s available for preorder now for a later October delivery at Best Buy, priced at $3,800.

周三公布新款Nvidia RTX Spark AI Windows PC规格的PC厂商不止Microsoft一家。Dell也公布了此前宣布的Dell XPS 16 Creator Edition PC的详细信息。该机型现已在Best Buy开启预售，10月下旬发货，售价3,800美元。
